ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

So we are looking at how the **उपनिषद्** is trying to make us understand **आत्मा**. **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः** **दर्शनाय** **श्रोतव्यो** **मन्तव्यो** **निदिध्यासितव्य**:. So you first understand what the **श्रुति** is saying. That is **श्रवणम्**.

Without adding my own prejudice to it. What I think it should be. This is not okay, this is how it should be — that **बुद्धि** I let go. This is what **श्रुति** says. I agree with it, disagree with it, it's a different matter. This is what **श्रुति** says, and I give it the benefit of doubt. But I am not able to accept it. It doesn't sound logical to me. Because my mind is different. I think it should be different. Who gave it at mind? The giver of the mind knows all the possibilities. If **भगवान्** wanted us all to conform with everything, he would have made us perfect conformers.

He would have made us like an animal. A cat will only mew, it won't bark. A human being will bark and mew and twitter also. It will do everything. Human being has all freedom. So if **भगवान्** wanted us to only mew, he would have made us like cats.

But free will is given. Freedom is total. Freedom to suffer is also total, freedom to be free is also total. That's human freedom. You have the entire gamut of freedom available to you. So **श्रुति** says — **श्रोतव्यः** — but my — logically, unless the mind accepts something, is able to find it reasonable, there will always be a thorn in the side. You hear it, alright, but it won't become my knowledge ever, because I can't understand why it is like this, why it is like this. What a defect! Faulty production **भगवान्** has made. You say **ईश्वर** is perfect — what perfection? He has given such a stupid mind, doesn't work properly. Why this nose doesn't smell things correctly? Why the eyes don't see how they should see? I can find hundred things which are seemingly wrong.

But all of those I have to understand. In my limited understanding it doesn't sound right. But in the overall scheme of things, that's the way it's designed. Because there is no real design. Okay. All that is there is one.

But if somebody is suffering, who is suffering? **परमात्मनस्तव**. You know — **परमात्मनस्तव** **अज्ञानबन्धात्** — you are a problem. You have a problem. So what should you do? You should just remove **अविद्या**. So really who is suffering? **परमात्मा** is suffering. **परमात्मा** is suffering? **परमात्मा** never suffers. Who is suffering right now? **परमात्मा**. What do you mean? **परमात्मा** who doesn't understand I am **परमात्मा** suffers. Therefore there is no suffering. There is no defect. Because there is only one reality. But until I see it, there are defects. There is **पुण्यम्**. There is **पापम्**.

So if you try to see it in that way, in the Vedic view, that everything is **कर्मफल** — everything that you are seeing, including how you feel about things in the world, is all given. That, that's the way your mind would feel, is given. So, Swami, I am perfectly okay. I can feel the way I do. You can, if you are happy feeling that way all the time. No, no, I want to feel different. You can't feel different unless you know different. You have to know. When we know it's the way it will be, it is the way it is, then I stop resisting it. So I must see that what is being said is reasonable. That is **मननम्** — with respect to **आत्मविद्या** here, obviously.

We don't do **मननम्** about, you know, you should — **ज्योतिष्टोमेन** **स्वर्गकामो** **यजेत** — then do **मननम्** to be done there. Do **ज्योतिष्टोम** to go to **स्वर्ग**. What **मननम्** will you do about it? There is **श्रवण** and then **अनुष्ठानम्**. That's the two steps. **श्रवणानन्तरं** **श्रद्धया** **अनुष्ठानम्**. Do what has to be done with **श्रद्धा** after that, in the right way. Dot every i, cross every t. Wife should come and look at the ghee before you put in the fire. She should look — don't ask. Make her look. That's the way it works. Okay.

But when it comes to **आत्मविद्या**, it is not about believing and doing something. It's not ending in belief. It is knowing what I am. And therefore **मननम्** becomes very important. So you have to be able to — mind should be able to say, yes, it's not unreasonable. It's not against reason. It's not within reason, it's not against reason. If it's within reason — **अनुमान**, **अर्थापत्ति**. If it's not against reason, **प्रमाण** is not **अर्थ**, **अनुमान**, etc. It's not opposed to reason. Because what I can't accept is what is opposed to reason. It need not be within reason, it must not be opposed to reason. See the distinction.

So that, seeing it that way, means what the **श्रुति** said becomes acceptable to the mind. Now major battle has been won. One major battle has been won. Despite seeing many, logically I have no reason to say there are many. But there is one more big hurdle. What is that? I don't feel that way. That means we have to work on it. That is called **निदिध्यासन**. Basically these are the three.

So **श्रवणम्** was already said here. By what? **ब्रह्म** **तं** **परादाद्** **योऽन्यत्रात्मनो** **ब्रह्म** **वेद**, **क्षत्रं** **तं** **परादात्**. We saw — two, four, six. Now where we have entered? We have entered into **मननम्**. What is **मननम्** now? How is **मननम्** being done? How there is only one is shown through **स्थिति**, **सृष्टि** and **लय**. Order is changed.

So we saw **स्थिति**. What was **स्थिति**? We saw **दुन्दुभि**. When you listen to the drum, unless you hear the drum you cannot distinguish the special notes of the drum. Unless you listen to the — unless you recognize the sound of a conch, you cannot know the special notes of the conch. The special notes of the conch are non-separate from the basic notes of the conch. Basic notes and special notes are here, not in the sound. Sound is only one. Therefore this — when you hear a conch, you hear a conch, special sounds of a conch, only when you see, hear the conch sound. You hear the conch sound only when you hear. You only hear when there is a mind also behind it. The mind is also there only when there's consciousness behind it. Therefore all the way, there is only consciousness.

It is not — there is not basic sound plus **शङ्ख** sound. Basic sound plus drum sound. There is no such thing called basic sound. What is basic sound? What is basic sound? What is advanced sound? Tell me. What do you understand by basic sound? **सामान्यम्** means what? This **सामान्य**-**विशेष** problem, addressing. **सामान्यम्** and **विशेष** — are they two things?

Normal fabric and my **अङ्गवस्त्रम्**. What distinguishes **अङ्गवस्त्रम्** from the fabric, you tell me? **अङ्गवस्त्रम्** is the name of what I am wearing. That which covers the body is called **अङ्गवस्त्रम्**. What is the difference between this and fabric? No. It's fabric. **विशेष** is **अङ्गवस्त्रम्**. **विशेष** is **नामरूपात्मकम्** purely, here knowledge. Therefore there is no **अङ्गवस्त्रम्** other than fabric. To see the fabric you must see the **अङ्गवस्त्रम्** — rather, to see the **अङ्गवस्त्रम्** you must be seeing the fabric.

Okay, I am taking it from the sound to the view. **तमेव** **भान्तमनुभाति**, **भान्तमनु** — there is the shining **चैतन्यम्**, after that only everything shines. You take it from any sensory perspective, from any thought perspective, ultimately everything you know is a thought, and every thought is that **चैतन्यम्**. It is not **साक्षी** plus a thought. That's the emphasis I am trying to make. There is no plus. It is plus as though. Where will you draw the line between awareness and a thought? Where will you draw the line? How will you draw the line? Where does awareness end? Where does thought begin? Can you have boundaries? Unless you can objectify awareness, you have no boundary. And you cannot objectify awareness.

Therefore, what am I? What am I? Oh, I am this man. Man is known to me. Before I knew also, I was there. What am I? Swami, I am consciousness. Where does it end? Physically, is there a limit to it? No. In space, is there a limit? No. In time, is there a limit? I was conscious, now I am not — you can't say that. I will be consciousness — you can't say that. Because a conscious fellow only can say. Therefore, where does it end? Suppose I am knowing a monkey and I am knowing a cow. Is consciousness different? Therefore, you see that you cannot — you cannot draw a boundary for where it is. Like you can't draw the boundary for the ocean versus the wave. But the **प्रतीति** that there is an ocean and a wave doesn't go away. But cognitively I can see that the wave is nothing but the ocean. Same is true here. When I say I have — I am hearing the sound of a conch — it's consciousness all the way. That's the method.
