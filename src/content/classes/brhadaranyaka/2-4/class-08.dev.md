ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥
We are looking at याज्ञवल्क्य telling his wife मैत्रेयी that he would like to move on from गृहस्थ आश्रम, seeks her permission and tells her — since I want to move on I will divide the wealth I have between you and your co-wife कात्यायनी so you can be comfortable. And मैत्रेयी asks — hey, will that wealth give me what you're going after? Will it give me that immortality, will it give me a मोक्ष that you're interested in? No. Then why are you giving me this? Why don't you give me what you are going after? Why don't you give me that knowledge? That's the section we are in. So last time we saw a lot of verses in the वार्तिक। In fact, I overshot what I had covered in the मूलम्। So we will finish that मूलम् today and then move to the next section. It's a story but still there's a lot to learn from the story.
So we saw — याज्ञवल्क्य said —
मैत्रेयीति होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यः; उद्यास्यन्वा अरे अहम् अस्मात् स्थानात् अस्मि; हन्त ते अनया कात्यायन्या अन्तं करवाणि इति॥ २-४-१॥
We repeat this so you get it in your head. मूलम् — I am going on to a higher आश्रम। हन्त ते अनया कात्यायन्या अन्तं करवाणि इति — I am going to put an end to the relationship between you and कात्यायनी because I am the bridge for you both to be related. I am going to do that for you. And divide the wealth इत्यर्थ:. So see — he just says I am going to put an end to the relationship. It is भाष्यकार who says that you are connected through me, and I will remove this connection, and I will distribute the wealth. How did भाष्यकार imagine this dividing of wealth etc.? Because he sees what's the next verse. It's not just imagination. It's a lot of looking at what was said and what is being said contextually.
So, what did she say in response? सा होवाच मैत्रेयी । यन्नु म इयं भगोः सर्वा पृथिवी वित्तेन पूर्णा स्यात्कथं तेनामृता स्यामिति नेति होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यो यथैवोपकरणवतां जीवितं तथैव ते जीवितं स्यादमृतत्वस्य तु नाशास्ति वित्तेनेति ॥ 2-4-2 ॥
 She is saying — if I have all this wealth, will I gain immortality? He didn't talk of giving wealth here in the first words. So how did she get that idea then? By looking at the answer, you understand what was communicated, whether it is said in so many words or not. When he says "I am going to put an end to the relationship between you two, I am going to take संन्यास" — she knows what he will do. He will put everything in their name and move on. So she is already seeing all that. That's perhaps the way the society was. It was well understood. Or the उपनिषद् did not give all the details. But the details can be figured out. Either way, you can look at it. So she asked this question. What the commentators do is — look at what is going to come, what is said elsewhere in the श्रुति, and based on all that, they write a commentary. So it's not just you look at the Sanskrit in that one line and you explain it. That's not enough. You have to have a vision of the whole to explain the part of it. And you must understand the parts to understand the whole. This is how the arrangement is.
So she asks — if you give me this earth surrounded by oceans with all the wealth in it, will I become immortal with that? Not directly with wealth, शङ्कर explained, but the wealth is a means to doing कर्म। So the question is, by doing कर्म, will I get immortality? And what did याज्ञवल्क्य say? नेति होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यः। It won't happen. Then why are you giving me all this wealth? It serves no purpose. No, it does serve a purpose. It does give you economic freedom. It does give you a comfortable life. So the life that people who are endowed with wealth would have, that kind of a life you will also have. But wealth — or through wealth — कर्म cannot give you immortality. यथैव उपकरणवतां जीवितं तथैव ते जीवितं स्यात्। अमृतत्वस्य तु न आशा अस्ति वित्तेन।
Then what happens? Then सा होवाच मैत्रेयी। Then मैत्रेयी responded again. What did she say? येन अहं न अमृता स्यां किम् अहं तेन कुर्याम्। With that which won't give me immortality, which won't make me immortal किम् अहं तेन कुर्याम्— what will I do with that? यदेव भगवान् वेद तदेव मे ब्रूही इति Whatever you know, O sir, my dear husband, give me that which will give me immortality. Whatever has made you go after संन्यास, you have a knowledge — that knowledge of immortality you want to abide in — that knowledge you give me. Don't give me this wealth. That's the real wealth. That real wealth you give me, because that we saw last time in the verses of the वार्तिक। That is the real wealth.
Also, I believe I had left one verse out. I will approach that after repeating the previous one. 
वित्ताच्चेदमृतत्वं स्यात्तत्तित्यक्षा न युज्यते ।। वित्ताच्चेन्नामृतत्वं स्याद्वद तेन ममापि किम् ।। ५९ ।।
So if wealth is going to give you immortality, then it doesn't seem appropriate that you are giving up your wealth. And if it doesn't give immortality, then what will I do with it too? Then the next verse — which is what I didn't do —
यद्यस्त्यनुजिघृक्षैवं करुणा वा मयीष्यते। यदेव भगवान्वेद तेन संविभजस्व माम्॥ ६०॥
If you have any desire to bless me, or any compassion toward me — then share with me that very thing which your honour knows.
I should have started here. यदि अस्ति अनुजिघृक्षा एवम् करुणा वा मयि इष्यते। यद् एव भगवान् वेद तेन संविभजस्व माम्। यदि अस्ति If you have anything — what do you have? अनुजिघृक्षा। अनुजिघृक्षा means what? अनुग्रहीतुम् इच्छा — a desire to bless, a desire to shower grace on me. If that is what you wish to really do, एवम् करुणा वा मयि इष्यते — or if you have compassion for me, if you care for me. See, these are all very nice words. If you tell a person, "if you care for me, do this" — what will he do? If he cares, he has to show the care in the way you expect it. Right? So if you have compassion for me — यद् एव भगवान् वेद — whatever the respected husband knows — तेन संविभजस्व माम् — that you share with me. Don't share some stupid post on Facebook. Share something useful, okay? तदेव संविभजस्व माम् — share that with me.
The rest of these verses we have seen. There are some other Vartika verses which we have to cover but first we have to cover through the मूलम्. We will spend some time there.
सा होवाच मैत्रेयी येनाहं नामृता स्यां किमहं तेन कुर्यां यदेव भगवान्वेद तदेव मे ब्रूहीति॥ २-४-३॥ स होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यः प्रिया बतारे नः सती प्रियं भाषस एह्यास्स्व व्याख्यास्यामि ते व्याचक्षाणस्य तु मे निदिध्यासस्वेति ॥ 2-4-4 ॥
सा होवाच मैत्रेयी।स होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यः - This is exactly is what you will start with if you go with संस्कृत भारती, these kinds of lines you will start saying. That conversation you will get. स means स: - He said. Who said?  याज्ञवल्क्यः said. Then she said. Who is she?  मैत्रेयी said — now what? सा होवाच मैत्रेयी That मैत्रेयि said; स होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यः That याज्ञवल्क्य said. उवाच you can translate as "responded" — she asked, he responded. That will make it in English the translation become more meaningful. If you just say "said" for उवाच, one word won't convey it sometimes. उवाच means "teach" sometimes; उवाच means "said" sometimes; उवाच means "asked" sometimes; उवाच means "questioned." All these translations have to change in our head in keeping with the context.
स होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यः So when मैत्रेयी asked this question येनाहं नामृता स्यां किमहं तेन कुर्यां— "what will I do with it? Please teach me that which you are going after, which is for immortality" — then याज्ञवल्क्य is moved. Because he now sees — he thought मैत्रेयि was perhaps not ready for this. He thought मैत्रेयि would like to live along comfortably. But when he hears this from her — or, he wasn't sure whether she was ready or not ready, he wasn't sure. So when you are not sure, you better err on the right side. Which means what? Don't tell, "come on, I will teach you this." You say, "you know, you enjoy life." "Hey, I don't want to enjoy life" — that should come from the student, not from the teacher. "You should give up everything and come and sit in the आश्रम्" — no teacher should say that. Because if he says that, he is putting the student in a spot — that unless you come and sit in the आश्रम्, you are not अधिकारी. "Come and sit here, take संन्यास।" Then what will happen? Even if the student is not ready, he or she may say, "okay, गुरु says I must be ready." It doesn't work that way. So याज्ञवल्क्य does not tell her, "come on, all this is useless. I won't give you wealth. I'll teach you." He doesn't say that. She has to ask. Unless asked, this विद्या should not be given. That's another convention. Even though कृष्ण knows भगवद्गीता all along, he did not ever teach अर्जुन voluntarily. Even in the गीता he did not start teaching until अर्जुन said “ शिष्यस्तेऽहं शाधि मां त्वां प्रपन्नम् - I am your शिष्य, please teach me." Then only he started. Here also — नामृता स्यां किम् अहं तेन कुर्याम्? What will I do? यद् एव भगवान् वेद तदेव मे ब्रूहि। ब्रूहि — tell, ब्रूहि — teach. Both can come. So teach me that. Because it means it has to be understood, not a matter to be believed.
स होवाच याज्ञवल्क्यः प्रिया बतारे नः सती प्रियं भाषस एह्यास्स्व व्याख्यास्यामि ते व्याचक्षाणस्य तु मे निदिध्यासस्वेति ॥ 2-4-4 ॥
Then याज्ञवल्क्य says, you know — प्रिया बत अरे नः सती प्रियं भाषसे। प्रिया सती नः प्रियं भाषसे। Being dear to me all along in my life, since I got married to you, being very dear to me — you speak what is dear to me even now. Being married, was one relationship. We had our social responsibilities together. We grew together in life. All that is wonderful. But, you know, I didn't expect that when I want to move on from this life of धर्म-अर्थ-काम, you would still be interested in this. I didn't imagine that. So you are very dear to me because you accompanied me.
What is पत्नी? धर्मपत्नी। पत्नी is one who is married through a यज्ञ, for the sake of doing a यज्ञ। That is what the definition of the word पत्नी is. How do we know that? पाणिनि says — पत्युर्नो यज्ञसंयोगे। One सूत्र। That the word पति itself, the feminine form of the word पति, is पत्नी। And when can you use that word? Not if you went and signed a register. Registered marriage does not entitle you to the word पत्नी। Then what entitles you? When the persons are brought together through a वैदिक यज्ञ। यज्ञसंयोगे। यज्ञेन संयोगे। यज्ञाय संयोगे। - ततपुरुष तृतीय & चतुर्थी both When the two are brought together by a यज्ञ for the sake of doing यज्ञs, then the lady is called पत्नी। So this part of life we lived well, and you have lived life very meaningfully with me. You have been supportive of me all the way. Because without her support, he cannot do धर्म। He needs her assistance. He needs her cooperation. He needs her to give a lot of leg room. She has to maintain the अग्निहोत्र fire. Not an easy job. So all this you have done. You have lived the role of a wife wonderfully. And therefore you are always dear to me. But now that I want to move on, it's the time to move on, you know. I'm cutting links in a way. But even now, when I want to move on for something which is more valuable, you also want something more valuable. Therefore you are not only dear until now, you know — प्रिया सती — you were dear — and you speak what is even more dear to me right now. प्रियं भाषसे। You speak what is really what I really love.
