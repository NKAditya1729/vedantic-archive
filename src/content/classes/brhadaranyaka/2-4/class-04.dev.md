Okay. So we are in the midst of the introduction to the fourth **ब्राह्मण**, the second **अध्याय**, called the **मैत्रेयी ब्राह्मण**। **मैत्रेयी** is the name of the person to whom the teaching is being taught. The teacher is **याज्ञवल्क्य**. And **याज्ञवल्क्य**, the story begins by **याज्ञवल्क्य** approaching his wife **मैत्रेयी**, seeking her permission to leave home and go for **संन्यास**. And the story ends with him going for **संन्यास**. He leaves the home and goes. So therefore what is the story meant for? Because the **शास्त्र**s may not say something explicitly. **शास्त्र**s may convey something, may not say it explicitly. Because story is there anywhere and story indicates something. So every time there is a story, the story is supposed to make it easier to learn because you forget everything else. Suppose you have been listening to Swamiji or even me for a length of time and I said Murari, you will know something, okay? I don't have to say anything more. The stories do a fantastic job. So **आख्यायिका**, a story is used by the **श्रुति**s as a means for easy communication.

So now what does the story communicate? So **शङ्कर** begins by saying, it says that **संन्यास** is a very important thing for knowledge to take place. That's what you should understand. It's not that **संन्यास** is necessary, but **संन्यास** is very important for a person who is trying to gain knowledge or trying to gain abidance in the knowledge. Because the knowledge that all there is is only me, after that there is no question of a choice to take something. If you have to say I want to do something for the sake of freedom, which means your freedom depends on the situation. If your freedom depends on the situation, then freedom is, we can go in the situation and change this. He took **संन्यास** and then next day **मैत्रेयी** went crying and said please come back. And he feels emotionally content and comes back. Then what will happen? He will say, oh **राम** went away. See **राम**'s story. He is supposed to go to the forest. He could have probably stayed comfortably. Quite near to **अयोध्या**, in the forest. But then what did all the people do? They went there. They would go there now and then. And then **भरत** comes with the whole army and says, **राम** please come back. Then **राम** says, if we stay here, we will not avoid trouble. See, **वनवासा** will not be **वनवासा**, because people will keep coming, and they'll bring **लड्डु** and give also. Please eat, you're staying here alone. So he doesn't want all that. So what should he do? He has to change his situation again. He has to again go somewhere else where people can't approach him.

So what I'm saying is, if we depend on the situation to make us feel okay, then the situation can again change. How much ever we bring a change, a change can again come. But if freedom depends on **ज्ञानम्**, and **ज्ञानम्** depends on what? **ज्ञानम्** depends on nothing but what is. If **ज्ञानम्** alone gives me freedom, because freedom is the truth, then no situation can take it away from me. But to facilitate that **ज्ञानम्**, there may be situations required to facilitate that **ज्ञानम्**. **ज्ञानाय**, you need **संन्यास** as a possibility for some person. But **ज्ञानादेव मोक्षः**, **ज्ञानमेव मोक्षः**. **न कर्मणा** — not by **कर्म**, but the way it is presented, we highlight something — so you highlight and say **संन्यास** is **मोक्ष**, it can be said somewhere **संन्यास** is necessary, so we should see if we already have a predilection to the thinking that **संन्यास** alone will free me, then that statement we will hold on as a truth, we will not take it as an indicator that it's very important. So we have to be very cautious. All over the **श्रुति** what is said, **ज्ञानमेव**, **ज्ञानादेव कैवल्यम्**। **ज्ञानादेव मोक्षः**. Yet there will be arguments that, you know, no, no, even though you have **ज्ञानम्**, unless you take **संन्यास**, you won't enjoy it. Which means you want to enjoy something. And which means you are depending on the mind again.

So I am just saying, we have to be very cautious with these things. You can choose to be free, you can choose to be bound. Who chooses to be bound? Who won't choose to be free if you expect choice? Won't work that way. Okay. So keep it in view. We'll see a lot of things because we are highlighting **संन्यास** here. So we should keep this much in view. Every classroom extra will be set so that we balance out. **मैत्रेय्यै च कर्मसाधन-रहितायै साधनत्वेन अमृतत्वस्य ब्रह्मविद्या उपदेशात्**, **वित्तनिन्दा वचनाच्च**; **यदि हि अमृतत्व साधनं कर्म स्यात्**, **वित्त-साध्यं पाङ्क्तं कर्म इति** — **तन्निन्दा वचनम् अनिष्टं स्यात्**। You have seen this. I will just go through it again to connect.

So, previous one was that look, this **याज्ञवल्क्य**, who knew things very well, **कर्मी सन्**, being a **कर्मी**, a **गृहस्थ**, he took **संन्यास**. So, it shows **संन्यास**'s importance. Okay. So, that sentence — that **संन्यास** is important even for a **ज्ञानी**. That's the idea there. But then, what would Pujya Swamiji likely say? He would say, **ज्ञानी**, but wants more clarity. Wants abidance, further abidance, further clarity. Therefore, it is **विद्वत् संन्यास**. If **विद्वत् संन्यास** is taken by choice, because it's a will-based action, I want to take **संन्यास**, then it has to be for the sake of **ज्ञान** clarity, not for **ज्ञानम्** per se. He has **ज्ञानम्**. He defeats everybody. You will see in the third chapter. Okay.

Now the second thing is, **मैत्रेयी**. **मैत्रेयी** — is she **ज्ञानी** or **अज्ञानी**? She is **अज्ञानी**. So even for **मैत्रेयी** who doesn't know, **कर्मसाधन-रहितायै**. She has no **कर्म साधन**. Why? She is a woman. **वैदिक साधन** is not there. And until she is married and her husband is alive, she has **कर्म** through her husband. Participating with her husband. Husband cannot do **अग्निहोत्र** without her. So whatever he does, 50% of all his **पुण्य** comes to her. And all **पाप** he does, 100% goes to him. And any **पाप** the lady does, that **पाप** 100% goes to man. Because he didn't direct her work. This is how Pujya Swamiji would say. I don’t know whether he meant seriously or half-jokingly. But the whole idea is that the wife who is totally under the direction of her husband, her only job is to take care of her husband. She gains everything in the process because of that attitude. That’s the way it is presented.

So then, **मैत्रेयी** is what? **कर्म साधन रहिता**. Because once **याज्ञवल्क्य** takes **संन्यास**, what **कर्म साधन** she will have? She doesn't have any independent **वैदिक कर्म साधन**. She can do **स्मार्त कर्म**. **स्मार्त कर्म** means what? **पूजा**, etc. That's possible. For that you don't need any **यज्ञोपवीत**. See, this is why sometimes people will tell that as a woman, you know, you can do **विष्णु सहस्रनाम**, you can do **ललिता सहस्रनाम**, you can do lot of prayers. Why should you chant **वेद**? This statement you will hear. That's because orthodox way of looking at it is — unless you have been initiated into **उपनयनम्**, you cannot chant **वैदिक मन्त्र**s. Therefore, **आचार्य**s who are orthodox will not teach **उपनिषद्**s in public. Because to teach **उपनिषद्**s, they must chant at least **शान्ति मन्त्र**. And they chant **शान्ति मन्त्र**, and you chant **शान्ति मन्त्र**, you will chant **वैदिक मन्त्र**. If you chant **वैदिक मन्त्र**, you should have been initiated to **वेद**. In a public setting, everybody may not be initiated. Therefore, we won't do it. Therefore you will teach **विवेकचूडामणि**. You will teach **अपरोक्षानुभूति**. Things which are having the same substance, summand substance of **वेद**, but not **वेद**. **वेद** directly is not to be even uttered by people not initiated. This is an orthodox view.

Swamiji would say, all that is true for **कर्म काण्ड**, but when you have **वैराग्यम्** for everything, then your **अधिकारित्वम्** is no longer having **उपनयनम्**. Your **अधिकारित्वम्** is your **वैराग्य**, your **विवेक**, your **मुमुक्षा**, your **शमादिषट्क सम्पत्ति**. That is what makes you a person eligible for **उपनिषद्**, not whether you are a man or a woman. **मैत्रेयी** also is qualified, otherwise he can't teach her. Kind of qualified.

So, **मैत्रेय्यै च**, **मैत्रेय्यै च**, **कर्मसाधन-रहितायै**, **साधनत्वेन अमृतत्वस्य ब्रह्मविद्या उपदेशात्**। So, what is **कर्म साधन** - he is teaching her? He is giving her knowledge. Therefore, what is a **साधन** for **मोक्ष**? It is **ब्रह्मविद्या**. Okay. Secondly, so we are saying **कर्म** is not a **साधन** for **मोक्ष**. Why? To show **कर्म** needs to be given up. Not only not a **साधन**, it will come in the way. Because your identity as a doer will come in the way of your understanding, "I am not a doer." Therefore, number one, **विद्या** is a **साधन** for **मोक्ष**. Because **मैत्रेयी** who has no **कर्म साधन** is being taught **विद्या**.

Secondly **वित्त-निन्दा-वचनाच्च**, with the **निन्दा वचन** itself. **निन्दा** means putting down. Wealth or means are put down. When **मैत्रेयी** asks, you are giving all this, leaving so much wealth to us, will this wealth give you the freedom you are going after? Answer is no, it cannot. Wealth cannot — wealth can only give you a comfortable life, it cannot give you **मोक्ष**. Therefore, and what is wealth meant for? Wealth is not meant for wealth's sake. Wealth is meant for doing **कर्म**. **वित्तम्** is a **कर्म साधनम्**, **जाया** — wife is a **कर्म साधनम्**, **पुत्र**, child, progeny is a  **कर्म साधनम्** — **उपासना**, knowing **उपासना**s is a **कर्म साधनम्**. So what is **कर्म साधन**? **जाया**, **पुत्र**, **वित्तम्** - both **मानुषम् वित्तम्** wealthand **दैवम् वित्तम्**. Therefore, **कर्म साधन**s are what? **जाया**, **पुत्र**, **वित्त** - wife, progeny and wealth (both human and **दैव**) and what is the **साध्यम्** ? **साध्यम्** will be **लोक**. **लोक** means a sphere of experience like heaven etc. What is a **साध्यम्** for a person who is interested in **मोक्ष**? **लोक** is always travel. You travel from one place to another. You are never free from traveling. Therefore, for one who says my **लोक** is myself — I don't want to travel anymore — he is called **आत्मलोकः**. So for such a person, what is the use for **वित्तम्**? What is the use for means when the end by which, by the end for which those means are required, I have no value for. If **लोक**, a sphere of experience is my end, then I need the means to gain that end, because that end has to be gained in time. But once I say, **आत्मा एव अयं लोकः**, this myself is my goal, the understood me, the me who is free, then where is the need for **वित्त**, wealth? Where is the need for wife? Where is the need for progeny? All those become redundant. Therefore, **वित्त-निन्दा-वचनाच्च** (**वचनात् - पञ्चमि**), on account of wealth being put down as a means, therefore what? It means that **कर्म** is not needed and in fact is to be dropped.

**यदि हि अमृतत्व साधनं कर्म स्यात्**। Now the other way round. If **कर्म** was, if action centered on the will of the person, was the means for **अमृतत्वम्** — literally translated immortality, our translation limitlessness — **अमृतत्व साधनम्**। If action was the means for limitlessness, **वित्त-साध्यं पाङ्क्तं कर्मेति** therefore for **कर्म** what do you need? You need means. Therefore wealth would become a necessity for **कर्म**, and **कर्म** being a necessity for **मोक्ष**, wealth would be necessary for **मोक्ष**. If wealth were necessary for **मोक्ष**, **कर्म**, which is **पाङ्क्तम्** in nature — **पाङ्क्तम्** means what? Depends on the **यजमान**, the **जाया**, etc. — fivefold factors. Then **तन्निन्दा-वचनमनिष्टं स्यात्** putting down wealth, which is equivalent to putting down **कर्म**, would not be appropriate. If **कर्म** were a means for **मोक्ष**, for limitlessness, then **कर्म** should not have been put down. And if **कर्म** is not to be put down, wealth which is required for **कर्म** should also have not been put down. But since wealth has been put down or will be put down in this **ब्राह्मण**, it indicates that **कर्म** is not a means for **मोक्ष**.

**यदि तु** — on the other hand. **तु** — on the other hand. That's how you learn Sanskrit, okay?
