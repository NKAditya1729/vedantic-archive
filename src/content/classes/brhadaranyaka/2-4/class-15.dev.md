ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

We are in the midst of the **मैत्रेयी** **ब्राह्मणम्**, where the question has been asked by **मैत्रेयि** — teach me that which gives **अमृतत्वम्**. And **याज्ञवल्क्य** began by saying things which are intended to make a person objective. Recognise that what you really love is only the pleased self. Everything that one does in life is because you want something that is dear to you. And anything is dear to you not for its own sake, but only for your own sake. And that "you" happens to be already pleased — you better know that. That's the idea. You better know that.

Therefore you should — this, the real you, the you who is free from a need to become different — needs to be recognised. **द्रष्टव्यः**. There's nothing more to be done. But to recognise, you have to do things. To know, though knowing appears to be doing — knowing is what? **वस्तुतन्त्र** — it is dependent on the object. Knowing depends on what you want to know. Knowing does not depend on your wish. Whatever we know is determined by what it is that we are trying to know. If I am knowing the sun, I will know it as a sun. If I am knowing a star, I will know it as a star. I cannot see a star and know it as a sun. So knowledge is true to what is to be known. And what about **कर्म**? Karma is **कर्तृतन्त्र** — it is true to what you want to do. It depends on you. What you want to accomplish depends on you. Something unaccomplished, to be accomplished, depends on you. But what is already a fact, which is to be known, depends on what is.

So the statement is — **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः**. And for that, what should you do? **श्रोतव्यः**, **मन्तव्यः**, **निदिध्यासितव्य**: What will happen if I do this much? You will know. Then what will happen? Then everything will become **आत्मा** for you — there will be nothing but **आत्मा**. That's the way **आत्मा** has to be known — not as an individual standing apart. This much has been said. Now what we are doing, since a couple of classes, we are going into more detail as to how the **वार्तिककार** presents it.

So last time we saw how **वैराग्यम्** is important. That was presented — saying, whatever begins will end. Whenever somebody climbs up, there will be a downfall. So you look at what happens: any change is subject to further change. Therefore understand that anything you do, accomplish, etc. — it doesn't matter what you accomplish, that accomplishment will wither away in time. Whatever is accomplished in time will disintegrate in time. Therefore understand that any attempt to change, any attempt to bring something, to accomplish something new — it's good, it's fine, but that can't be the last word. Because what is subject to change will change again. Therefore have **वैराग्यम्** towards trying to accomplish anything new.

And not accomplish — what is it? You think that by accomplishing this, I will become okay. By this relationship I will become okay. This will keep me secure. This idea is not true. Because again and again you notice in your own life — you examine your own life — again and again we see that we thought something would solve my problems, I will be okay after that. But it's like an ever‑moving target. And the world promotes this, because that's the way they make you do what they want you to do right now.

Okay, come on — you are enjoying life, playing all the time happily. Suddenly the 10th standard comes. And then you are told, come on, very important — study, study. One year you study, after that you will be good. Very untrue, you know. But we are all taken for a ride. Then we study hard. Then what happens now? 10th you did so well. Now you set a new standard for yourself. Now you have to keep up that standard. 12th also you will do equally well. And what your mother and father couldn't accomplish, they want you to accomplish — because, you know, they didn't do very well sometimes. So they say, you should become a physician. Why? Because I didn't become one. I want to enjoy myself by making you one. Now you are set up. You struggle hard. Just two more years, okay — after that you will have the whole life to enjoy. Two more years you study, then what happens? You are somehow made to a college — are you going to just walk out of the college without doing anything? No. Somehow you study for four years, you finish that. Then what happens? Are you done now? No, no. Now you have to get an MD seat. You study some more. Are you done with that? No, no. Now you have to do post‑MD, or get a job. Where does it end? We are on a permanent, eternal trip of becoming better. That is called **संसार**.

Until I am happy to keep on seeing my growth and keep doing better, it works well. But at some point you hit a plateau. You recognise that whatever I do, it's like the horse running to get that grass which is tied in front of its face. Poor thing — I don't know if it's so foolish, but that's how human beings think the horse runs, because it wants to reach that grass in front, and the grass is always maintained at that distance, therefore it is always running. This is how life tends to work.

Therefore — anything that you depend upon for you to become okay, that dependency will not give you what you are really seeking. Therefore you don't love a wife for the wife's sake — you love her for your own sake. You don't love a husband for the husband's sake — you love him for your own sake. You don't love children for the children's sake — you love them for your own selfish sake. You don't love wealth for wealth's sake — you love wealth because you think that is what makes you happy. Finally, it is you. You don't love **लोक**, experiences, for the experience's sake — you love them for your own sake. Even if you want to experience some feeling of high, whatever it might be. So if everything is for your own sake — who is this very demanding "you"? You try to know it, and you find it is non‑demanding. This is the thing. So this **वैराग्यम्** we have seen.

Now the next word that is going to be looked at is — **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः**. So what is this **आत्मा**? Last time, towards the end, we saw — everybody knows **आत्मा**. Who doesn't know oneself? Anybody here who doesn't know oneself, who has come to class? You are, and you want to do something — that's why you came. You come first. Before you do anything, before you know anything, you are. Is there a doubt? Nobody has a doubt. Therefore — why do I need the **वेद** to come and tell me **आत्मा** should be known? Give me a break, I know. Therefore, this **आत्मा** should be known — what **आत्मा** is to be known? First question.

I, the wanting fellow, have to know — yes, that I am wanting. I am a wanting fellow, that's what I need to know. That is the **द्रष्टव्य**? Can't be. Therefore, what did **सुरेश्वर** say? It cannot be the known **आत्मा** that you want to know — no **वेद** is required to come and tell you that. It is the unknown **आत्मा**. The unknown "I"? Yes. The unknown you. That is what has to be known.

So there is an unknown me? Yes. There is an unknown you. Known‑you and unknown‑you are fused. There is no two you's — there is only one fused you. A confused you. That is called **तादात्म्यम्**. There is no second thing. There is only a wanting me. There is no "sometimes I become free from want" — generally I am wanting. This is the only "I" I know. So when I am wanting, I am wanting only; when I am not wanting, I am not wanting only. There is only one me, fused — and that fused is confused. Because, yes, there is only one thing — but the only one thing that is, is the free you. That's not how I see it.

So first we have to make — we have to put a wedge — to show there is "you" what you think you are, and there is something more about you. That is the first step of **विवेक**. You have to separate what you think you are, this personality, from something which is more real than the personality. Then — what is more real than the personality? And finally you say, there is no personality, there is only that, that's all there is. This is the whole journey. But to make that split, and to be convinced that the personality is depending upon the real me — that the whole universe depends on me — is a big leap we made.

You can't — suppose you want to jump over to **लङ्क** like **हनुमान्** **जी**. You better be sure that when you jump, you will land in **लङ्क**. Otherwise you will fall in between. You can't say, half I will jump, I will jump in two steps. First step — where will the first step be? You will be in the ocean. Then you cannot have a second step. Therefore you must be able to jump. But here we cannot jump like that, because the leap is too big. There is a method to it — because you already are in **लङ्क**. You just have to know "I am already in **लङ्क**, I don't need to jump." That's the method.

So now what we have to see is — what is this **द्रष्टव्यः**? The section we are going to look at now — it may not look immediately relevant sometimes, but we have to see how the discussion is relevant. Because the question is this: how do you accomplish this **द्रष्टव्यः**? Is it a **विधि**, an injunction? Injunction means a command — do it, do it, know it. The general thinking is, anything to be accomplished, you need to put an effort into it, you need to work for it. Work only produces what is not there. A pleased you is not there right now — if he were there, he wouldn't come to this class. Therefore the pleased you has to be gained. By what? By doing something.

Even now, look at our own mind: "Swami, what should I do for this **मोक्ष**? What should I do?" No, you don't have to do anything, you are already free. "No, no, what should I do?" You have to know. "You told me I know it already." No, that is not knowing. So this "what should I do" business is a disease we all suffer from. It's a universal disease, because we only know one thing to get things done — do. What is already there, I don't need to do. I will not go and ask **भगवान्**, give me a hand with five fingers. If I want, I'll ask for a sixth finger — give me one extra thumb on this side also, then I have two thumbs, very nice, it'll be interesting. I can ask that. But I cannot go and tell **भगवान्**, give me a hand with five fingers — I already have one. I can say, give me an extra hand with five fingers. So you need to understand what you can ask for, what you can accomplish, and what is already accomplished. If something is already accomplished, it has to be known.

But the universal thinking is — anything in life, even small ends, you have to work to accomplish. **कर्म** is the means to accomplish. So if you want to have one house of your own, what do you need? You need to do, you need to accomplish it. Suppose you need a second house, a farmhouse — you need to work even harder. Suppose you need five houses — work even harder. Suppose you want to become a minister — work harder. Suppose you want to become the Prime Minister — it's not going to just fall in your lap, you need to work for it. So the more difficult the accomplishment, the higher the accomplishment, the more you need to work for it. Logically correct or not? Therefore, if you want to gain everything in the universe — how much work should you do? Infinite. Now, that infinite is too much. So give me an **उपाय**, a trick by which I can reduce the work and get it done. So the **वेद** will give you some trick — this is the way we look at it.

So these things are all true within a certain realm. Within the finite‑mathematics world, finite‑mathematics principles work — 1 plus 1 is 2, 2 plus 1 is 3, this is finite mathematics. What is infinite mathematics? Infinite plus 1 is infinite. No addition. You can't apply the rules that work in the realm of finite math to infinites — won't work. What works in Newtonian physics won't work in quantum physics — a paradigm shift. The paradigm shift here is — what you take yourself to be is not what you are. That's the paradigm shift. If you want to retain your identity and get better, that's within the paradigm. We don't know of another paradigm, and therefore there is no understanding of a paradigm shift. Therefore we try to fit in the paradigm shift into the current paradigm, and when you try to do that, you get frustrated, because it doesn't give you what the paradigm shift will give.

Understand the importance of **विधि**. The idea is, the **वेद** is a book of means and ends. It has to tell me new means and new ends, which I cannot know within my range of perception. And when it gives me a new end, I must apply the means — if unknown means are given, I must apply them to get that end. And if it's a known end but I have a new trick, a new means to get there, I should apply that. No effort, no result; no work, no pay. Therefore — what should be done? Tell me, tell me what should be done. That is the fellow. That's where we are, generally. See whether we fit there or not. What are we trying to do to gain this freedom — trying to do, or trying to just know? Where do we stand? That's going to be the discussion. I hope you will also enjoy this discussion.

So we saw this last time —

आत्मा प्रत्यक्षप्रसिद्धेः स्यात् तत्रैव आत्मानुभूतितः। इति प्रमेयनिर्देशो द्रष्टव्य इति तत्प्रमा।।

आत्मबुद्धिरियं तावत् सर्वेषां जायते स्वतः। अप्राप्ता सर्वात्मता त्वेति सैवातो धीर्विधीयते॥

So — **आत्मबुद्धिः** **इयम्** **तवत्**, this sense of "I", **सर्वेषां** **जायते** **स्वतः**, for everybody it is there inherently, naturally. There is nothing to be known there. That is true, you know yourself. But you know yourself as an isolated individual. **अप्राप्ता** **सर्वात्मता** — "all that is here is me is **अप्राप्ता**, **अज्ञाता**” therefore, **सा** **एव** **अतः** **धीः** **विधीयते** — that alone (that knowledge) is being enjoined here.

Now —

द्रष्टृद्रष्टव्ययोर्भेदे सत्येवं धीर्विधीयते। नियोज्यविषयाभेदे घटते न विधिर्यतः॥ ९७ ॥

When can you give a command, that you need to change, that you need to accomplish something? **सुरेश्वर** says: **द्रष्टृ**‑**द्रष्टव्ययोः** **भेदे** **सति** — if there is a gap - if what is to be accomplished is totally different from the accomplisher **द्रष्ट** the seer (**द्रष्टृ**) I am using the word "accomplish" because the question is in that frame: when can you mandate that something must be done by you? When the doer is separated from what he will accomplish by doing.

So now — what is the word? **द्रष्टव्यः**. It means "should be seen." That's it. The **तव्य**‑**प्रत्यय**, language‑wise, can mean a command, an injunction. And the **पूर्वमीमांसक** will argue that **तव्य** means it's a **विधि**, a command, an injunction — because it wants me to do something. He is very committed to saying the **वेद** only tells me to do or not do; there is no business of just knowing. Therefore, this gap — **द्रष्टृ**‑**द्रष्टव्ययोः** **भेदे** **सति** if there is a gap between the two **एवं** **धी** **विधीयते** — then you can have an injunction, "you should know.” **नियोज्य**‑**विषय**‑**अभेदे** **विधिः** **न** **घटते** **विधिर्यतः** But where what is to be mandated and what is already there have no gap between them — no space, no differentiation between the knower and what is to be known — there cannot be a **विधि**.

So what?

**उत्तरोत्तरभूयांसि** **नामादीनि** **यथाक्रमम्।।** **उपन्यस्याऽऽत्मयाथात्म्यं** **तत्तत्वं** **प्राब्रवीच्छ्रुतिः।।** **९८** **।।** Now **सुरेश्वर** is taking us to **छान्दोग्य**, **सप्तम** **अध्याय** — the seventh chapter. There **सनत्कुमार** is approached by **नारद**. **नारद** says what? Sir, I have heard that the knower of **आत्मा** is free from sadness - **आत्मवित्** **शोकं** **तरति**, the knower of **आत्मा** crosses sorrow; so please teach me that. **सनत्कुमार** says — first tell me what you know, then I will teach you; because I need to know where you are to progress. Progress means - were we on **मन्त्र** 2.4.5 or 2.4.6 I need to know before I progress. Let me know how much you have studied; what do you know? **नारद** said — you want to hear, okay — and he started, and he won't stop. He goes on and on — **ऋग्वेदः**, **यजुर्वेदः**, **सामवेदः**, **अथर्ववेदः**, **छन्दः**, **शिक्षा**, **कल्पः**, **निरुक्तम्**, **ज्योतिषम्**, **नक्षत्रविद्या**, **क्षत्रविद्या** — going on and on. After he finishes — five minutes he took — **सनत्कुमार** said, is that all you know? "Yes sir, that's all I know." And that me, who knows all this — **सोऽहं** **भगवो** **शोचामि** — that kind of me, O sir, is grieving, is unhappy, is wanting, still wanting. What should you do? **शोकस्य** **पारं** **तारयतु** — help me cross over this ocean of sorrow.

**सनत्कुमार** says — what you know is only **नाममात्रम्**. You only know names; you don't know the thing. So now he has to make a big leap, and that big leap, in that **उपनिषद्**, is not done abruptly — it is done in a particular way. There he first gives an **उपासना** based on **नाम**; from **नाम** he goes to the next step, and like this he keeps going — from 15, 20 steps he goes. Saying, what do you want to know next? You have known names — name after name after name. Now what do you want to know next? Because knowing a name won't help you. He doesn't ask him — he tells him. So he is taking him through a progression. Then he has to present the limitless:

उत्तरोत्तरभूयांसि नामादीनि यथाक्रमम्। उपन्यस्याऽऽत्मयाथात्म्यं तत्तत्वं प्राब्रवीच्छ्रुतिः॥ ९८ ॥

**उत्तरोत्तर**‑**भूयांसि** **नामादीनि** **यथाक्रमम्** **उपन्यस्य** - **उत्तरोत्तर** — in a sequence one after the other; **उपन्यस्य** — having presented them; **नामादीनि** — beginning with **नाम**; **यथाक्रमम्** — in the right sequence; **आत्म**‑**याथात्म्यम्** **तत्**‑**तत्त्वम्** **प्राब्रवीत्** **श्रुतिः** — then he described what you are really to **नारद** the **श्रुति** did that through **सनत्कुमार**, the teacher.

भूम्नश्च लक्षणं चक्रे यत्र नान्यदिति स्वयम्। अथ यत्रेति चाल्पस्य प्राणान्तस्य च लक्षणम्॥ ९९ ॥

**चक्रे** - “did” - **कृ**-**धातु** - **लृट्** **लकार्** - **आत्मनेपद** . **भूम्न**: **लक्षणं** **चक्रे** - Then what did **श्रुति** do? It gave **भूमा** **लक्षणम्** - **भूमा** is what? **भूमा** is **ब्रह्मन्** because that **इमनिच्** **प्रत्यय** will come and then **बहु** will become **भुमा** - **बहुवत्** **भाव**: **भुमन्**. Therefore, **भूमा** means the limitlessly big, the limitless whole, **ब्रह्मन्**. **भूम्नश्च** **लक्षणं** **चक्रे** as what? **नान्यत्** **इति** **स्वयम्** - What is this **नान्यत्** business we will look at.

Okay, we are doing **बृहदारण्यक**, but now we will do **छान्दोग्य**.

यत्र नान्यत्पश्यति नान्यच्छृणोति नान्यद्विजानाति स भूमाथ यत्रान्यत्पश्यत्यन्यच्छृणोत्यन्यद्विजानाति तदल्पं यो वै भूमा तदमृतमथ यदल्पं तन्मर्त्यꣳ स भगवः कस्मिन्प्रतिष्ठित इति स्वे महिम्नि यदि वा न महिम्नीति॥ १ ॥ (छा ७.२४.१)

Scene change. So now, what happens? **यत्र** **नान्यत्पश्यति** **नान्यच्छृणोति** **नान्यद्विजानाति** **स** **भूमा** — what are we trying to show? Keep the context in view. The first statement is: a **विधि** is only possible when what is to be accomplished is different from the accomplisher. Now, what do we want to show? What does **सुरेश्वर** want to show? There is no accomplisher and accomplished at all — there is one whole. This is what he wants to present. And once it is proved, this is what the **श्रुति** is saying: your **विधि** has no place; it just goes out of the window.

So I can't just make a statement and say "okay, therefore it's not a **विधि**." I have to prove that the **वेद** is only presenting **आत्मा** as one whole. That's what he is presenting here. **बृहदारण्यक** is going to do the same; but since we are talking **बृहदारण्यक**, he is going to other **श्रुति** — hoping, probably, that you have studied **छान्दोग्य** first. I don't know. The way the teaching works is always like this: the first round you study, everything looks hazy, because any quotation is equally unknown to me. The second time you study, the quotations start to make more sense. The third time you study, they start sitting in. That's the way it works — multiple rounds of the whole study help. So here, this is the way we get multiple rounds: in between you will get this, then sometime else you will get this, then one day you will study this section, and it will all fit, fall in place.

So now, what is **भूमा**? Where? **यत्र** **न** **अन्यत्** **पश्यति** — you don't see another; **न** **अन्यत्** **शृणोति** — you don't hear another; **न** **अन्यत्** **विजानाति** — you don't know another. Means what? There is no another. That understanding, where there is no second thing, alone is **भूमा**. And **अथ** **यत्र** **अन्यत्** **पश्यति** — where you see another — **अन्यत्** **शृणोति** — you hear another — **अन्यत्** **विजानाति** — you know another — **तदल्पम्**: that is limited, **अल्पम्**. It is insignificant, because there is another. If there is another, it means you are as good as your body‑mind‑sense complex, nothing more than that. Your house, your kingdom, etc. is not you — you can be pulled out of there anytime. Therefore you become restricted to this much alone. How big is it going to be? Six feet? Seven feet tall? Three feet wide? Four feet wide? Beyond that, do you know anybody — even if he is a basketball player or a football player? That much. That is why they say, how much space do you need to be buried? That much. That much is you — that is **अल्पम्** — very small, very limited. The tree which you planted has become bigger than you. You planted a small mango tree, now it's huge; the number of mangoes it produces is heavier than you. You are **अल्पम्** only — what else will you be? So, **तदल्पम्**.

**यो** **वै** **भूमा** **तदमृतम्** - what is **भूमा** is **अमृतम्**; you want **अमृतत्वम्**, **मैत्रेयि** wants **अमृतत्वम्** — and **अथ** **यदल्पं** **तन्मर्त्यम्** — whatever is small,**अल्पम्** is in time, is **मर्त्यम्**. This is what he taught. So this is quoted to say — this **भूम्नः** **लक्षणं** **चक्रे**, saying "**यत्र** **नान्यत्** **न्यत्पश्यति** **नान्यच्छृणोति**…” and where you see another **यत्र** **च** **अन्यत्** **श्रुनोति** **तद्** **अल्पम्** — and what is that **अल्पम्**? Everything other than **आत्मा** is **अल्पम्**, **प्राणान्तस्य**.

अथात इति वाक्येन ह्यहमेवेति निश्चितेः।। निषिध्यानात्मरूपं यत्ततोऽहंकारनिह्नुतिः।। १०० ।। (वार्तिक)

स एवाधस्तात्स उपरिष्टात्स पश्चात्स पुरस्तात्स दक्षिणतः स उत्तरतः स एवेदꣳ सर्वमित्यथातोऽहङ्कारादेश एवाहमेवाधस्तादहमुपरिष्टादहं पश्चादहं पुरस्तादहं दक्षिणतोऽहमुत्तरतोऽहमेवेदꣳ सर्वमिति॥ १ ॥ (छा ७.२५.१)

अथात आत्मादेश एवात्मैवाधस्तादात्मोपरिष्टादात्मा पश्चादात्मा पुरस्तादात्मा दक्षिणत आत्मोत्तरत आत्मैवेदꣳ सर्वमिति स वा एष एवं पश्यन्नेवं मन्वान एवं विजानन्नात्मरतिरात्मक्रीड आत्ममिथुन आत्मानन्दः स स्वराड्भवति तस्य सर्वेषु लोकेषु कामचारो भवति अथ येऽन्यथातो विदुरन्यराजानस्ते क्षय्यलोका भवन्ति तेषाꣳ सर्वेषु लोकेष्वकामचारो भवति॥ २ ॥ (छा ७.२५.२)

तस्य ह वा एतस्यैवं पश्यत एवं मन्वानस्यैवं विजानत आत्मतः प्राण आत्मत आशात्मतः स्मर आत्मत आकाश आत्मतस्तेज आत्मत आप आत्मत आविर्भावतिरोभावावात्मतोऽन्नमात्मतो बलमात्मतो विज्ञानमात्मतो ध्यानमात्मतश्चित्तमात्मतः सङ्कल्प आत्मतो मन आत्मतो वागात्मतो नामात्मतो मन्त्रा आत्मतः कर्माण्यात्मत एवेदं सर्वमिति॥ १ ॥ (छा ७.२६.१)

Now, when you say this **भूमा** is everything, still the tendency is — there is "something else", a **भूमा** —**यत्र** **न** **अन्यत्** **पश्यति** because **पश्यति** is third person, and what you see has **सकर्मक** it should to be other than you. If you see an **आत्मा** other than you, it will still be limited. Therefore the **श्रुति** doesn't stop there. It goes on and says: **स** **एव** **अधस्तात्** — that alone is below — **स** **उपरिष्टात्** — that alone is above — **स** **पश्चात्** — that is behind — **स** **पुरस्तात्** — that is in front — **स** **दक्षिणतः**, **स** **उत्तरतः** — south, north. All directions are covered, space is covered — **स** **एवेदं** **सर्वम्** — that alone is everything. But **सः** is again third person. So you think it's something else? No. Now he changes — **अथ** **अतः** **अहंकारादेशः**. The method of teaching is this: first you present the third person; then what? **अहमेव** **अधस्तात्** — **सः** is replaced by **अहम्** — I alone am below, I alone am above, I alone am behind, I alone am in front, I alone am in the south, I alone is all this - **अहमेवेदं** **सर्वम्**. But you think now — this "I", the I which says "I am a man", that I. So the **श्रुति** thinks — the **श्रुति** is very careful — "anything I tell you, you will put it back into your old paradigm; I need to fix this." It can make the attempt — okay — but it can't make me change paradigms. So what does it say now? **अथ** **आत्मादेशः** — **आत्मैव** **अधस्तात्**.

First third person (**सः**), then **अहंकार** (**अहम्**), then **आत्मा** (**आत्मादेशः**) — everything is **आत्मा**. So, for a person who knows this, who understands this — that **आत्मा** is in all directions, **आत्मा** alone is everything — what is remaining? For such a person: **तस्य** **ह** **वा** **एतस्य** **एवं** **पश्यतः** **एवं** **मन्वानस्य** **एवं** **विजानतः**. See three words — **पश्यतः**, **मन्वान**:, **विजानतः** — you can see how they parallel **श्रोतव्यः**, **मन्तव्यः**, **निदिध्यासितव्यः**. For such a person: **आत्मतः** **प्राणः**, **आत्मत** **आशा**, **आत्मतः** **स्मरः**, **आत्मत** **आकाशः**, **आत्मतस्तेजः**, **आत्मत** **आपः**, **आत्मत** **आविर्भावतिरोभावौ**, **आत्मतोऽन्नम्**, **आत्मतो** **बलम्**, **आत्मतो** **विज्ञानम्**, **आत्मतो** **ध्यानम्**, **आत्मतश्चित्तम्**, **आत्मतः** **सङ्कल्पः**, **आत्मतो** **मनः**, **आत्मतो** **वाक्**, **आत्मतो** **नाम**, **आत्मतः** **कर्माणि**, **आत्मत** **एवेदं** **सर्वम्**.

This is the sequence (reversed) through which **सनत्कुमार** took him. He began with **नाम**. **नामादि** — and from **नाम** he went on. **नाम** means words; words are nothing but sounds — **वाक्**; rewind, and **वाक्** has no being other than mind — therefore **मनः**. That's the sequence there. So you resolve words into sounds, sounds into mind, the vacillating mind into **सङ्कल्प**, **बुद्धि**; from **बुद्धि** you go to a composed mind, **ध्यानम्**, etc. — a sequence. So he is saying: once you finish the journey, now what do you say? All that is me. All that which you saw other than you is you. Now what remains? **आत्मतः** **कर्माणि**, **आत्मत** **एवेदं** **सर्वम्** — everything came from **आत्मा**. Means what? There is no other thing except **आत्मा**. And if there is no other thing except **आत्मा**, where is **विधि**, sir? Where is the difference for you to enjoin (**विधि**) about **आत्मा**? There is nothing but **आत्मा** — that is what it is saying.

आत्मैवेत्यादिवाक्येन पूर्णब्रह्मावबोधिना। नाविध्वस्तं तमोऽतोऽस्ति न च ज्ञानमनुत्थितम्॥ १०१ ॥

**अथ** **इति** **वाक्येन** **अहमेव** **इति** **निश्चितेः** — that is the **अहंकारादेश**; **अनात्मरूपं** **निषिध्य**, having negated the not‑self form, **ततः** **अहंकारनिह्नुतिः** — then the **अहंकार** itself is denied. So the **अहंकार** is also denied by saying **आत्मैव** **सर्वम्**. Therefore he says, by the sentence **आत्मैव** **इत्यादि** **वाक्येन**— **आत्मैव** **सर्वम्** -"**आत्मैव** **सर्वम्**" **इति** **वाक्येन** **पूर्णब्रह्मावबोधिना** — by that sentence, which wakes you up to the fact that you are that **ब्रह्मन्** that is the whole **न** **अविध्वस्तं** **तमः** **अस्ति** **न** **च** **ज्ञानम्** **अनुत्थितम्**—when this is understood then there is nothing which is unknown to you; **तमः** the darkness, the **अविद्या** about anything which doesn’t exist and there is no knowledge which has not happened for you. Not that you now know everything — I told you, you don't know Russian, you don't know French — but you know that everything is me - **सामान्यम्**

नानिवृत्तस्तथाऽनर्थो नानवाप्तं सुखं तथा। कार्यज्ञेयसमाप्तिः स्यात्प्रत्यग्याथात्म्यबोधतः॥ १०२ ॥

**न** **अनिवृत्तः** **तथा** **अनर्थः** — once you know **आत्मा**, there is nothing that you do not want that you are freed from - whatever you don't want, you are already freed from it. **न** **अनवाप्तं** **सुखं** **तथा** - and don't think it's only in a negative way; there is no joy that you have not gained, because all joys are you; everything is you. Any effects to be known — it's over; the journey of wanting to know something else is also over. **कार्यज्ञेयसमाप्तिः** **स्यात्** — the end of all **कार्य** (to‑be‑done) and all **ज्ञेय** (to‑be‑known). Why? **प्रत्यग्याथात्म्यबोधतः** — by knowing yourself, the real nature of the inner self, as you are.

सर्वासूपनिषत्स्वेवं प्रमात्रादेरनात्मनः। प्रत्यङ्मात्रैकयाथात्म्यं श्रूयते सोपपत्तिकम्॥ १०३ ॥

**सर्वासु** **उपनिषत्सु** — in all the Upaniṣads **एवम्** in this manner — **प्रमात्रादेरनात्मनः** — Very interesting where it begins. Where does the world begin? Where does anything you want to know begin? With the **प्रमात्रा** the knower — "I know the world." That fellow itself is the imposter. Beginning from the **प्रमात्रा**, the problem begins with "I am a knower, and there is something other than me to be known” - **प्रत्यङ्**‑**मात्र**‑**एक**‑**याथात्म्यम्** and therefore, what do you hear? You hear only that which is **प्रत्यक्** **आत्मा** **श्रूयते** and how? **सोपपत्तिकम्** — along with the logic to assimilate it.

इहापि नेति वाक्येन प्रतीचोऽतत्स्वभावकम्। व्युत्थापितमनूद्याऽऽह सर्वमात्मेति नः श्रुतिः॥ १०४ ॥

**इहापि** — even here, **सुरेश्वर** has come back to **बृहदारण्यक**, **इहापि** **नेति** **वाक्येन** - **नेति** **नेति** we saw … **प्रतीचः** **अतत्स्वभावकम्** **व्युत्थापितमनूद्याऽऽह** **सर्वमात्मेति** **नः** **श्रुतिः** Here also, by showing you what? By saying not this, not this - **प्रतीचः** **अतत्स्वभावकम्**. This **प्रत्यगात्मा** is different from anything that can be known.
