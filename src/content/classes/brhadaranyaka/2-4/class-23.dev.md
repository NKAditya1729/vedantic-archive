ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

So we were in the middle of the **मैत्रेयी** **ब्राह्मण**. And in that, it was said that everything one really loves is for the sake of oneself alone. And that — you think that that loved self comes and goes, it's changing — that is wrong. You need to understand what the self really is. It is not — it's different from what you are — it is you, but what you take yourself to be is one aspect of it alone. It's not the whole truth. Therefore, that **आत्मा** is **द्रष्टव्यः** — should be known. And for that — **श्रोतव्यः**, **मन्तव्यः**, **निदिध्यासितव्यः**. Not any one of them independently — all three together constitute the means. That's the **तात्पर्य** here. Because otherwise somebody says — we have three paths now. **श्रोतव्यः** — one path. **मन्तव्यः** — another path. **निदिध्यासितव्यः** — third path. Hey — there are no three paths. All three are meant for one — **द्रष्टव्यः**. To be known. To be seen. So therefore — only one goal. To know myself as I am. To know the truth as it is. Truth is only one. It cannot have paths. Knowledge does not prove paths. But **अधिकारित्वम्** — there are many ways to get there. Possibilities. But **ज्ञानम्** is non-negotiable. There is only one way — is to know the thing as it is. **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः**. All right.

Then we saw the next **मन्त्र**, which is what — this **ब्रह्म**-**क्षत्रिय** etc. He said — whatever you say — in any identification with ourselves, if we negate our identity with the whole, but take on an identity with anything else — that limited identity to which we subscribe — that very thinking that "I am limited to this identity" denies me the whole. So if one says — I am a **ब्राह्मण**, and that **ब्राह्मणजाति** is different from **ब्रह्मन्** — which means, if you see anything which is more than one entity in reality, and you hold on to one entity — holding on to that one entity denies me the completeness which I really am. I am the whole — that it denies. Therefore — **ब्रह्म** **तं** **परादाद्योऽन्यत्रात्मनो** **ब्रह्म** **वेद** । **क्षत्त्रं** **तं** **परादाद्योऽन्यत्रात्मनः** **क्षत्त्रं** **वेद** । **देवास्तं** **परादुर्योऽन्यत्रात्मनो** **देवान्वेद** । — like this, for each one of them. Every one of them — you look and say — if you hold on to any one identity and think it is different from the whole — there are distinct things — then that distinct understanding that you are holding on negates or denies you your wholeness. The **ब्राह्मणजाति** does not — does not disown you really — you disown yourself. Being said in that way — that if you say — I am a **ब्राह्मण**, I am not anything else — then that will deny you. Because you are saying **ब्राह्मणजाति** is different from others — it means there are many realities. That will deny you. So this was said. What is said really? There is only one reality. Ultimately, that is what is being said there. Okay?

यो न वेदात्मयाथात्म्यं वेद जात्यादिमत्तया । स यद्यतोऽतो जातिस्तं परादध्याज्जडं नरम् ॥

**यो** **न** **वेद** **आत्मयाथात्म्यम्** — one who does not know himself as he really is. **यो** **न** **वेद** — then — but how will you know? **वेद** **जात्यादिमत्तया**. **जात्यादिमत्तया** — third case. **जात्यादिमत्ता** — so the **प्रातिपदिक** is **जात्यादिमत्**. What is **जात्यादिमत्**? **जात्यादि** — **आदिपदात्** you can bring **लोक**, **देव** — everything — whatever else you want to bring in. That — having that — **मतुप्** **प्रत्यय** — **जात्यादिमत्**. Then — **तस्य** **भावः** — having that, you know, the identification — there is — **जात्यादिमत्ता** — "ness" — **जात्यादिमत्ता**. Then — **तया** - **जात्यादिमत्तया**. So what does it mean? And he says **जडं** **नरम्**— very interesting — because anything that you objectify is **जडम्**. Whatever you objectify is **जडम्**. It is not **चैतन्य** — it is not consciousness. What cannot reveal itself is **जडम्**. Does another person reveal himself to you? No. Does any object reveal itself to you? No. Do your eyes reveal themselves to you? No. Does your mind reveal itself to you? No. You reveal everything. You are the only one who is **चैतन्यम्**. Everything else is **जडम्**. So — that's a better definition of **जडम्**. Because otherwise — struggle to show why, you know, one thing is **जडम्**, one thing is **चैतन्यम्**. There is nothing. Because all **चैतन्यम्** — any life that you see anywhere — is borrowed from that one reality. Borrowed — rather, manifestation of that one reality. Okay — so he is saying that if you identify with anything — **तं** **जडं** **नरं** **परादध्यात्** — that fellow is as good as a stone, because he is not thinking. He is negated. What is the **श्रुति** revealing? **इदं** **ब्रह्मेदं** **क्षत्त्रमिमे** **लोका** **इमे** **देवा** **इमानि** **भूतानि** **इदं** **सर्वं** **यदयमात्मा** — this — all — **श्रुति** has to reveal: everything is **आत्मा**. What else has to be said? Job over. So **श्रवणम्** is over. **श्रवणम्** is over. I understand what **श्रुति** says. But how? I don't see it. Logically it doesn't fit. All right — **मननम्**. Okay. So till now — so what we have seen in the 2.4.5? **श्रोतव्यः**, **मन्तव्यः**, **निदिध्यासितव्यः**. What is **श्रोतव्यः**? This **मन्त्र**. Now **मन्तव्यः** has to begin. All right? Okay.

So then he saw — **यस्मात्** **आत्मनो** **जायते** — so what is it? Everything is **आत्मा**. Why do you say everything is **आत्मा**? **यस्मात्** **आत्मनो** **जायते** — because everything is born of **आत्मा**. **आत्मन्येव** **लीयते** Everything resolves into **आत्मा**. **आत्ममयं** **च** **स्थितिकाले** Everything is **आत्मा** even in **स्थितिकाल**. **आत्म**-**व्यतिरेकेणाग्रहणात्** , **आत्मैव** **सर्वम्** Since nothing is seen other than **आत्मा** — **आत्मा** alone is. कथं पुनः इदानीम् इदं सर्वमात्मैवेति ग्रहीतुं शक्यते ?

How — how is it possible now? **इदानीम्** — **स्थितिकाले**. Okay — **इदानीम्** is now — **स्थितिकाले** — bring that in. **कथं** **पुनः** **इदानीम्** — how is it possible to grasp, to understand, what you are saying — that everything is **आत्मा**? Before I was born, I don't know. After I am dead, I don't know. But now — I cannot accept it. If you want me to have **श्रद्धा** — everything is **आत्मा** — I can believe when I die I will become everything — that is not what is talked here. **स्थितिकाले** — how? **भाष्यकार** says —

चिन्मात्रानुगमात्सर्वत्र चित्स्वरूपतैवेति गम्यते । तत्र दृष्टान्त उच्यते — यत्स्वरूपव्यतिरेकेणाग्रहणं यस्य, तस्य तदात्मत्वमेव लोके दृष्टम् ।

This sentence should be well understood. **यत्स्वरूपव्यतिरेकेण** **अग्रहणं** **यस्य** — without which, if a thing cannot be understood, cannot be known, cannot be grasped — then that is its **आत्मा**. Statement. Without clay you cannot know a pot; therefore clay is **आत्मा**. Without gold — knowing gold — without seeing gold you cannot see a bangle or a necklace. **यत्स्वरूपव्यतिरेकेण** — without — **व्यतिरेकेण** — without — without seeing gold — **अग्रहणं** **यस्य** — **कटकस्य**, **कुण्डलस्य** — **अग्रहणम्** — you cannot see the bangle, you cannot see the earring, you cannot see the nose ring, you cannot see the necklace — without seeing gold. You cannot imagine the pot — the clay pot — without thinking of clay. You cannot see my t-shirt without seeing cloth — fabric. You cannot see a book without seeing paper. Any number of examples. And whatever you cannot see without seeing which — that is the **आत्मा**. **इति** **लोके** **दृष्टम्** — this is seen. It is common knowledge. It is not something exotic. **लोके** **दृष्टम्**.

So similarly — so I first covered the last sentence, okay — because I feel that is the center of the whole thing to be understood. **चिन्मात्रानुगमात्** **सर्वत्र** **चित्स्वरूपत्वम्** **अवगम्यते**. **चिन्मात्रानुगमात्** — so **अनुगम** is what? **अनुगच्छति**. So when you see a pot — clay — pot, you know, is there only when there is clay. Already. **भाति** — **अनुभाति**. **गच्छति** — **अनुगच्छति**. So — **चिन्मात्रानुगमात्** — anything you see, anything you know, follows you — **चिन्मात्र**. First — I am. Without "I am" there is no **व्यवहार** possible. No transactions are possible. Seeing, knowing, thinking, hearing, doing, dancing — anything you take — I am. And what is "I am"? Is it revealed to you by somebody else? "Yes, you are" — oh, you told me, therefore I am? No — doesn't work that way. How do you know you are? I don't care who you are — **संसारी** or **असंसारी** — how do you know you are? **स्वतःसिद्ध**. There is no doubt about it. Even for a cowherd who has never gone to school — he doesn't have to consult somebody — the cow — hey, I am there or not? The cow knows "I am". Man also knows "I am". There is no doubt there. Why? Because I am first. Then only everything else. So what is coming first? **किं** **भाति**?**चिन्मात्रम्** Everything else is **अनु**. So **चिन्मात्रानुगमात्**— without being this **चित्**, you cannot know anything. But you can be without even subject-object being recognized — like in sleep, or like between two thoughts. There is no activity in the world — you are. You know — there is no thought — that "you" is what — without that, can you ever be? **चिन्मात्रानुगमात्** **सर्वत्र** — in every situation. **सर्वत्र** — **सर्वदेशेषु**, **सर्वकालेषु**, **सर्वावस्थासु**. **सर्वत्र** **चित्स्वरूपता** **एव** **इति** **गम्यते**. Therefore you can know that everything is **चैतन्यम्**. Because **जड** goes, **जड** comes — but **चैतन्य** always is. **तत्र** **दृष्टान्त** **उच्यते**

And to make — because — what are you doing now? **शास्त्र** has told you what it has to tell you. But you are saying — no, no — how can it be? **कथं** **पुनः** **इदानीम्** **इदं** **सर्वमात्मैवेति** **ग्रहीतुं** **शक्यते** ? How I can say everything is only one? All right — you have a problem with your thinking mind. Right? You have a logic which says — how can it be? I will give you the logic. So — though you are seeing a chain, a necklace, a bangle and ten other things — you can still see one gold or not? And you can't count gold as one, and then bangle number two, necklace number three. You can't count like that. That's not counting. You have to count members of a set. Once you count gold, that set is swallowed. Similarly — once — if you count **चित्स्वरूप**, you can't count two. If you understand. If you think — no, no — **साक्षी** — **चित्** — everything else is **साक्ष्यम्** — you are in trouble. The **साक्षी** word has a lot of trouble in that sense.
