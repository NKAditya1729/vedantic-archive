    <p class="src-bhashyam">अविद्यायाश्च विषयः — ‘<span class="src-pratika">अन्योऽसावन्योऽहमस्मीति न स वेद</span>’ (बृ. उ. १ । ४ । १०) इत्यारभ्य चातुर्वर्ण्य-प्रविभागादि-निमित्त-पाङ्क्तकर्म-साध्य-साधन-लक्षणः बीजाङ्कुरवत् व्याकृताव्याकृतस्वभावः नाम-रूप-कर्मात्मकः संसारः ‘<span class="src-pratika">त्रयं वा इदं नाम रूपं कर्म</span>’ (बृ. उ. १ । ६ । १) इत्युपसंहृतः</p>
    <p class="src-bhashyam">शास्त्रीय उत्कर्षलक्षणो ब्रह्मलोकान्तः, अधोभावश्च स्थावरान्तोऽशास्त्रीयः, पूर्वमेव प्रदर्शितः — ‘<span class="src-pratika">द्वया ह</span>’ (बृ. उ. १ । ३ । १) इत्यादिना । एतस्मादविद्याविषयाद्विरक्तस्य प्रत्यगात्म-विषय-ब्रह्मविद्यायाम् अधिकारः कथं नाम स्यादिति — तृतीयेऽध्याये उपसंहृतः समस्तोऽविद्याविषयः ।</p>
    <p class="src-bhashyam">चतुर्थे तु ब्रह्मविद्याविषयं प्रत्यगात्मानम् ‘<span class="src-pratika">ब्रह्म ते ब्रवाणि</span>’ (बृ. उ. २ । १ । १) इति ‘<span class="src-pratika">ब्रह्म ज्ञपयिष्यामि</span>’ (बृ. उ. २ । १ । १) इति च प्रस्तुत्य, तत् ब्रह्म एकम् अद्वयं सर्वविशेषशून्यं क्रिया-कारक-फल-स्वभाव-सत्य-शब्द-वाच्य-अशेष-भूतधर्म-प्रतिषेध-द्वारेण ‘<span class="src-pratika">नेति नेति</span>’ (बृ. उ. २ । ३ । ६) इति ज्ञापितम् ।</p>

ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥

ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥

ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥

ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

So we are just poised to enter the fourth **ब्राह्मण**, **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**। So in the beginning we will look at the connection **भाष्यकार** gives to the **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**। We will find that **भाष्यकार** has to summarize whatever has gone by, all important things. And he summarized — every time you see a beginning, it's interesting to see how he summarizes, so it helps us remember what is important also, what we have to keep in view, what he has talked about so far in the whole **शास्त्र**। So today — you know, happens to be also **अक्षयतृतीया** — it is still running till 7:37 or so in the morning. So I said we should start today so that let there be **अक्षयफलम्** for us. You know, we are studying about **अक्षय** only anyway. That's why I thought I should start today or Thursday. So then I thought I will start today so we can get going. So with the prayer that all of us have a fruitful study, a good understanding and assimilation of the **शास्त्र**, we will begin the **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**।

So, **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** is the 4th **ब्राह्मण** in the 2nd **अध्याय** and it is called — the entire 2nd chapter is called **मधुकाण्ड**, 3rd chapter is called **मुनिकाण्ड। मधुविद्या** is the next section 2-5, but the entire chapter is called **मधुकाण्ड**।

So this is the **सम्बन्धम्**।

आत्मेत्येवोपासीत; तदेव एतस्मिन् सर्वस्मिन् पदनीयम् आत्मतत्त्वम्, यस्मात् प्रेयः पुत्रादेः — इत्युपन्यस्तस्य वाक्यस्य व्याख्यानविषये सम्बन्धप्रयोजने अभिहिते — 'तदात्मानमेवावेदहं ब्रह्मास्मीति तस्मात्तत्सर्वमभवत्' (बृ. उ. १।४।१०) इति; एवं प्रत्यगात्मा ब्रह्मविद्याया विषय इत्येतत् उपन्यस्तम्।

**आत्मेत्येवोपासीत** — where is this 1.4.7? We spent a lot of time there. Because this **आत्मेत्येवोपासीत** is considered like the sentence which is being explained starting from there till the end of **बृहदारण्यक**। So this word **आत्म इति एव उपासीत** — what is **आत्मा** there? **आत्मा** is not a body. **आत्मा** is what? **यदाप्नोति यदादत्ते यत् च अत्ति विषयान्** — that which pervades — basically, it's talking of what? **सृष्टि-स्थिति-लय-कारणम्** — so it is that from which it arises, that by which it is sustained, that into which it resolves — that's the definition of **आत्मा** coming from **लिङ्गपुराण**। But that is **आत्मा** here. What is this **इति** for **आत्म इति एव उपासीत**? That **इति** is presented as trying to show that it is different from what is **व्याकृत** and **अव्याकृत**। What is different from **कार्य** and **कारण**? What is different from **विदित, अविदित**? That **इति** is doing all that work for us. Because otherwise, see — the word **आत्म इति** means it looks like a typical **उपासनवाक्य**। So, **शालग्रामं विष्णुः इति उपासीत** — **शालग्राम** is not **विष्णु**। A stone is not **विष्णु**, a stone is not **शिव**। You will say what? In that you bring **शिव-बुद्धि**। You bring **विष्णु-बुद्धि**। Therefore this sentence is very easily misunderstood as an **उपासन-वाक्य**। But there the **इति** is not to say — it is not — you look upon it — that is not the meaning of **इति** here. Meaning has to be brought out. **सम्प्रदाय** does that. **एव** — **एव** is what? There is no other way. You know — it is not anything else. It is not **वाच्य**। It is not **अभिधान**। It is not **अभिधेय**। It is not a word. It is not the meaning of the word. Okay. So, we have to understand the word very clearly — what is being talked about is that which cannot be objectified. And **उपासीत** — **उप** is **सामीप्यम्** — near — **आस्** is to sit. What is nearer than **कार्य** and **कारण**? What is nearer than anything in the world? What cannot become nearer? That is **उप**। Same **उप** is in **उपनिषद्। उप** - **समीपे**। Means — this **उपासीत** means you do **विचार**। It does not mean **उपासन** — the sentence is very — if you look at it per se, it doesn't say anything. Without a commentary you would probably never understand it. It looks like it's stuck in a large **मन्त्र**. 1.4.7 is a big **मन्त्र**. It's called **विद्यासूत्र। आत्म इति एव उपासीत** is called **विद्यासूत्र**। So this was said already — **आत्म इति एव उपासीत** — so do enquire into this **आत्मा** yourself. What is the reality of yourself? With the help of **आगम-वाक्य**। Why should you look into it? We don't know the business. We can do a lot of things. **अग्निहोत्र** is there — we have done.

**तदेव एतस्मिन् सर्वस्मिन् पदनीयम् आत्मतत्त्वम्। तदेव** — That alone — **पदनीयम्**। What is **पदनीयम्**? **पद** is **पद्यते इति पदम्** — whatever you go after, **पुरुषार्थ** — what is sought after is called **पद। तदेव आत्मतत्त्वं पदनीयम्**। Amongst all things, anything that one can go after — what is the thing that really is worth going after? **अनीयर् प्रत्यय** has that meaning of extra force — it should be gone to, it's worth going after. **तद् अर्हति** — **तदेव एतस्मिन् सर्वस्मिन् पदनीयम्** — amongst all this, this is the one, it should be gone to. What is it? **आत्मतत्त्वम्**। Why should we go only after **आत्मतत्त्वम्**? Why not something else? **यस्मात् प्रेयः पुत्रादेः** — **प्रेयः** — what is **प्रेयः**? Dearer. It is dearer. It is **प्रियतरः। प्रेयः** — **प्रियतरः। प्रेष्ठः** — **प्रियतमः**। Should remember — this is **ईयसुन्प्रत्यय। प्रेयस्**। Like **ज्यायस्। ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन** — what is **ज्यायसी चेत्**? — if you think **कर्म** is better — **श्रेयश्च प्रेयश्च मनुष्यमेतः** in **कठोपनिषद्। श्रेयस्** — so that **ईयसुन्प्रत्यय** — **ईयस्**। Here also it is there — **प्रेयस्। प्रेयस्** means dearer. So, sometimes it appears it is better to use that relative degree word than the superlative. See — suppose you say "This is the dearest" — it's something absolute. But another way of saying it is — **प्रेयः** — anything you say, it is dearer than that. Anything you say, it's dearer than that. Which means what? You cannot reach it in any categorical way. Because anything you look upon, it is dearer than that. So in that way **श्रुति** sometimes plays with the words. You will see a lot of these relative words. You will also see the absolute sometimes also. **प्रेष्ठः** also you will see, but here **प्रेयः** — compared to what? **पुत्रादेः**। What is dear to one — house is dear to one, car is dear to one, job is dear to one, wealth is dear to one — I don't mind spending all my wealth because my children — I will take care of them. My children are so important, they are my life. **पुत्रः** — **पुत्रादेः अपि प्रेयः** — **आदिपदात्** — bring everything else. Children are dear to me and I am willing to do so much. I am willing to sell my house for the sake of the child. I am willing to, you know, spend all my money for a child's well-being. But then this body becomes more important than even that. For this body's sake I will give up something else too. I will even give up myself — if I can't take care of myself, I will take care of myself first. What is dearer than the body? What is dearer than life? What is dearest is myself. Whatever makes me comfortable, whatever pleases me. So, for some people, you know, dying for the cause of religion or dying for the cause of nation is more important than preserving this body. But even for them, what pleases them is most important. So, if you take it anywhere — what makes me more acceptable to myself is the dearest. Therefore, what is — see, whatever pleases me is dear to me. Whatever pleases me is dear to me. So, what is really what should be dear to me? What pleases me is dear to me secondarily — because it pleases me. Therefore, what am I really after? I am not after what pleases me. I am after the pleased I. Pleased me is what I am interested in. Happy me, a comfortable me, an acceptable me is what my real value is for. So therefore **प्रेयः पुत्रादेः** — it is that which is dearer than anything. Therefore **आत्मा** is dearest. **आत्मा** — self — self-acceptable — is the nature of **आत्मा**। It is by itself the **स्वरूप** of **आत्मा**। It is **प्रेयः। पुत्रात् प्रेयः, वित्तात् प्रेयः**। We add the word "pleased" because to us it looks like self can be pleased or displeased. Self can be happy or unhappy. Wanting or fulfilled. So I am not interested in wanting self. I am interested in fulfilled self. But finally what we understand — self is fulfilled. The problem is you take yourself to wanting, which is not inherent to self. Therefore, self alone is what we are looking for in and through every effort and pursuit in life.

Therefore, **आत्मतत्त्वं पदनीयं यस्मात् प्रेयः पुत्रादेः — इत्युपन्यस्तस्य वाक्यस्य व्याख्यानविषये सम्बन्धप्रयोजने अभिहिते** — this **उपन्यस्तस्य** — it is mentioned, it is said as **विद्यासूत्र**। Of this sentence, **व्याख्यान-विषये सम्बन्धप्रयोजने अभिहिते** — **अभिहिते** was said. What was said? **सम्बन्ध** and **प्रयोजन**। What is **प्रयोजन**? Purpose. What is **सम्बन्ध**? Connection between **विद्या** and **प्रयोजन**। And what are these? These are things to be explained. **व्याख्यान-विषये। व्याख्यानविषये सम्बन्धप्रयोजने अभिहिते** — Where was it said? **तद् आत्मानम् एव अवेद्** — he knew himself — as what? **अहं ब्रह्मास्मीति**। I am **ब्रह्मन्। इति** he knew. And when he knew, what happened? **तस्मात् तत् सर्वमभवत्** — when he knew **ब्रह्मन्**, he became everything. How he became everything? He didn't become — he recognised: I am **ब्रह्मन्** and **ब्रह्मन्** is **जगत्कारणम्**, therefore I am all that is here. He knew. So, this is what now — what is the **सम्बन्ध**? By knowing **आत्मा** alone you become — you are everything. There is nothing. There is no **द्वैतम्**। You become free. That is the **सम्बन्ध**। What is the **प्रयोजन**? **सर्वमभवत्**। You see you are all-pervading. There is nothing but you. There is no reason for fear because **द्वितीयाद् वै भयं भवति** — there is no second thing. There is no second reality. There is only one reality and everything is just a manifestation of that. **इति** — **एवं प्रत्यगात्मा ब्रह्मविद्याया विषयः**। In this way — **प्रत्यगात्मा** — to know **ब्रह्मन्**, what should you finally know? You cannot know some unknown **ब्रह्मन्** just like that. **ब्रह्मन्** is not one more thing to be known. Therefore, **ब्रह्मन्** can only be known — not as any one thing — but as all that is here. How would you know that? — only by knowing yourself as non-separate from that **ब्रह्मन्**। Therefore, **प्रत्यगात्मा**, I who is not objectifiable ever, never externalizable, that **आत्मा** alone is **ब्रह्मविद्यायाः विषयः**। That is the subject matter for **ब्रह्मविद्या। ब्रह्मविद्या** is **आत्मविद्या**। Because it began with what? **आत्मेत्येवोपासीत** — **न तु ब्रह्म इति एव उपासीत**। I am the subject matter of **ब्रह्मविद्या** — why? Because **ब्रह्म** is **आत्मा**। Therefore the knowledge is — I am **ब्रह्मन्** — **अहं ब्रह्मास्मि**।

And therefore, the knowledge is — I am **ब्रह्मन्। अहं ब्रह्मास्मि**। And by knowing that, you become everything — means what? There is nothing else to be gained. **ब्रह्मविदाप्नोति परम्**। Why? Because if there is one more thing, then there is something else remaining. But if — by knowing **ब्रह्मन्** — **तस्मात् तत् सर्वमभवत्** means **ब्रह्म एव इदं सर्वम्**, **सर्वं खल्विदं ब्रह्म** — this understanding is gained. **इति एवं प्रत्यगात्मा ब्रह्मविद्याया विषय इत्येतत् उपन्यस्तम्। भाष्यकार** is saying this all has been said already. It's been talked about. Further — you told what is really **पदनीयम्**, what is really to be gone after, the most important thing. But what about other things?

अविद्यायाश्च विषयः — the subject matter of अविद्या। What is अविद्या? अविद्या is not really knowledge — it can be improved upon. Because what is अविद्या? That which will go in the wake of विद्या। Okay. So, because there is no such thing, you can never objectify अविद्या। Whatever you objectify at that time is विद्या। Only when you understand it — as wrong — then विद्या will displace अविद्या। So, अविद्यायाः विषयः। अन्योऽसावन्योऽहमस्मीति न स वेद — so this is 1.4.10. Starting here, it's called अविद्यासूत्र। विद्यासूत्र is आत्मेत्येवोपासीत। Now अविद्यासूत्र is what? अन्योऽसौ अन्योऽहमस्मि इति — that is different, I am different — which means I have managed to contain myself, limit myself to this body-mind-sense complex. But it does say **त्यागेनैके अमृतत्वमानशुः** — and what did **भगवान्** say in Gita to praise **सन्न्यास**? What did he say? He said — **यस्तु कर्मफलत्यागी स त्यागीत्यभिधीयते** — to show the importance of **कर्म-फल-त्याग** — because there is no **सन्न्यास** without **कर्म-फल-त्याग**। What is the essence of **सन्न्यास**? Not **कर्म-त्याग। कर्म-त्याग** is by choice. **कर्म-त्याग** is by will. I choose to not do. I can choose to not do because I am lazy. I can choose to not do because there is a lot of effort involved. I can choose to not do by doing. So — you do, do it because it is to be done. Therefore, that is presented as the **सात्विक-त्याग** in Gita। **सात्विक-सन्न्यास** is what? Do **कर्म**, but do it because it is to be done. With no commitment, no excess value. Commitment is for the outcome. I am not doing **कर्म** without keeping the outcome in view. **कर्म** is being done for outcome's sake. But I am not committed to outcome — I am committed to what is to be done — that is **धर्म**। Doing **कर्म** in that way is called **त्याग। यस्तु कर्मफलत्यागी स त्यागीत्यभिधीयते**। So for a person, **त्याग-सन्न्यास** is important because **त्याग** in a way shows the **वैराग्यम्**।

Because I say I don't care for all this but I hold on to everything. Means what? Means I have some value for it. Because **वेद** allows you to walk away. If **वेद** allows me to walk away, who should walk away? **शास्त्र** presents two kinds of people who can walk away. One is **विद्वत्-सन्न्यासी**। One is **विविदिषा-सन्न्यासी। विद्वत्** means what? "You know." Then why take **सन्न्यास**? It's actually your life itself is **सन्न्यास**, whether you do it or not. **विविदिषा** is — you want to know, or you want to gain **निष्ठा**। For that sake you take **सन्न्यास**। Because you don't want to have — see, once you have responsibilities then in that **कर्म-फल** there would be some **आसक्ति** potentially. Because I am responsible. I cannot drop roles totally. And to take on a role is to again identify with the role. To identify with the role is to identify with the body-mind-sense complex. To identify with that is to go against **ज्ञान** — in a way. Therefore, how do you gain that abidance knowledge? For abidance, you go away. You may not — I mean, in **योग-वासिष्ठ**, will talk of this cycle very nicely. You start with being very engaged in the world. Then you discover **वैराग्यम्** for the world. And you throw everything in the world — "nothing, nothing." Then you understand the **ज्ञानम्**। And after the **ज्ञानम्** grows on you, you embrace the world back — with no problem. Because now it doesn't stick to you. This is the way **योग-वासिष्ठ** presents it.

But here, right now, in this context — since याज्ञवल्क्य is taking सन्न्यास, asking for सन्न्यास permission — and his end result is he went, means he took सन्न्यास — same याज्ञवल्क्य who is going to teach मैत्रेयी — ब्रह्मविद्या — why did he take सन्न्यास? Because सन्न्यास is an अङ्ग for ज्ञानम्। But that idea is being presented. Therefore, भाष्यकार introduces this, you know, section by saying — अस्या ब्रह्मविद्याया अङ्गत्वेन सन्न्यासो विधित्सितः — विधित्सितः — it is ordained. It is desired to be ordained. विधि is what? An ordain. A command — do/don't do. विधित्सितः means what? It is desired to be ordained. विधातुम् इष्टः। इच्छितः। विधातुम् इच्छितः। It means you desire to ordain. विधित्सितः। What is desired to be ordained? सन्न्यासः। As what? अङ्गत्वेन। For what? ब्रह्मविद्या। ब्रह्मविद्याया अङ्गत्वेन सन्न्यासो विधित्सितः। Why? जाया-पुत्र-वित्तादि-लक्षणं पाङ्क्तं कर्म अविद्या-विषयं यस्मात् न आत्म-प्राप्ति-साधनम्। Because जाया — wife? पुत्र — son — वित्त — money — आदिपद — bringing whatever else is needed for that — all of that is what it is — अविद्या-विषयम्। It's all within अविद्या। पाङ्क्तं कर्म। Therefore it cannot be आत्म-प्राप्ति-साधनम्। What is आत्म-प्राप्ति-साधनम्? It is क्रिया-कारक-फल-स्वभाव-सत्य-शब्द-वाच्य-अशेष-भूत-धर्म-प्रतिषेध-द्वारा — the gateway to ज्ञानम् is प्रतिषेध of all विशेषाः। And that means you remove this विशेष of कारक and फल also. When that is so, this जाया-पुत्र etc. is all within अविद्या। And therefore — we have to present the ज्ञानम् alone as साधनम्। But where is सन्न्यास? To that ज्ञानम् — that is a necessary अङ्ग। इति — भाष्यकार is introducing it, and we will come back in the next class.
