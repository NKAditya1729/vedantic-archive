ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

We are looking at the **भाष्यम्** of the fifth **कण्डिका**, the fourth **ब्राह्मण** — the **मैत्रेयी** **ब्राह्मण** of **बृहदारण्यक**। So what was presented is — **मैत्रेयी** said, don't give me wealth, give me real wealth. The wealth you are after, the freedom you are after — what will you give it to me? Then he invited her to come and listen — I will teach you, you listen. He started with what? — A wife is not loved, you know, for the wife's sake; she is loved for one's own sake. That's from the husband's side. A husband is not loved for the husband's sake; he is loved for one's own sake, for the wife's own sake. A child, a progeny, is not loved for the progeny's sake; the progeny is loved for one's own sake. Wealth is not loved for the sake of wealth; wealth is loved for one's own sake. **लोकाः**, **वेदाः** — anything you love is not for that. You see it as a means for you to be pleased. Therefore, what you are really interested in, what you really love, is the pleased you.

Okay? So what? Therefore, that's what is worth understanding. What is the nature of the pleased you? I know the nature. If you knew, I wouldn't tell you — you have to know it. If you already know, **श्रुति** has no job. **श्रुति** is a **प्रमाणम्**। It has a job only for **अज्ञातज्ञापनम्** — **प्रमाण** **लक्षणम्** - **अज्ञातज्ञापितम्** what is not known is made known by which, that is called a **प्रमाणम्**। And **श्रुति** is a deliberate **प्रमाणम्**। Deliberate means — it's not something you just get; it's deliberate in the sense that every word must have a meaning. What do I mean by that? My ears hear a lot of things; many of them I ignore, they're useless to me. Most of them are useless, very few things are really useful. Same thing — you see a lot of things, very few things are really required by you. But when it comes to **शास्त्र**, you cannot say many of them are useless. Every word in the **शास्त्र** must be meaningful — **प्रयोजनवत्त्वम्**। That **प्रयोजनवत्त्वम्** is the main important factor. It should serve a purpose. Otherwise it becomes purposeless — **प्रमाणान्तर**-**अनधिगतम्** and should be **प्रयोजनवत्**. Therefore, sometimes we say **अर्थवद्बोधकत्वम्** — we shorten it, **अर्थवत्**; **अर्थः** is **प्रयोजनम्**, not "meaning" necessarily. So it should serve a purpose.

Therefore, if **श्रुति** says **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः**, what does it indicate? What you think you know about yourself is still not really known — not adequately known. Yes, body is you — true, we are not questioning that — not enough, not adequately known. **असमग्रा** **विद्या** is **अविद्या**। Therefore, **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः** — **आत्मा** should be seen. How? How to see? Because I am, I know I am — "I am a useless guy, I know." No, that's not true. How will you know? **श्रोतव्यः** — it has to be listened to. What has to be listened to? What is you — the determination has to be listened to. How is the listening to be done? Listening is to be done with a view to understand what is the **शास्त्र** trying to convey to me about myself. What is this **तात्पर्यम्** of the **शास्त्र**? **श्रुति**-**तात्पर्यम्** **किम्**? It wants to convey something, and what it wants to convey I want to grasp. I am not interested in going with a conclusion and finding things to support my conclusion. Very, very big difference it makes. If I go into the **शास्त्र** looking for something to support my own conclusion, then **शास्त्र** is not a **प्रमाणम्**। **तर्क**, my own logic, becomes **प्रधान**, and the **शास्त्र** becomes secondary. You want the **शास्त्र** right, Ok, I will give you one - support what I have to say. I have already decided.

It's like the modern business houses. If they want to do something, it's not uncommon that you hire a high-priced consulting firm to study the problem and come up with recommendations. And a smart consulting firm knows that you should never give an advice which is not sought. So what should you do? Find out what does the fellow really want. He wants to lay off 10,000 people. Okay. So you spend one month studying — don't give it just like that. And then you come up with your research conclusion — recommendation is 10,000 people should be laid off. Now the management will say, "this is what Anderson Consulting has to tell us, and therefore, you know, we have no choice but to lay off people — we are very objective." The fact is they already know what they want to do, and they find a consultant whom they'll pay and he will scratch their back for them. This is not uncommon. This is how the world works.

Same way, if you approach the **श्रुति** already saying, "I know what I want to conclude" — now I will say **अन्नमयः** **आत्मा**, I told you body is **आत्मा**, look, **श्रुति** also says **आत्मा** is **अन्नमयः**। Hey — what's the next line after that? Did you read? **अन्योऽन्तर** **आत्मा** **प्राणमयः**। **श्रुति** is saying **अन्नमयः** **आत्मा** — I don't want to go further, because that's where I want to stop. This is not **श्रवणम्**। What is **श्रवणम्**? **श्रवणम्** is listening to the **शास्त्र**, keeping the **शास्त्र**-**अर्थ**-**निर्णय** basis in view. It is the **षड्लिङ्ग** — **उपक्रम**, **उपसंहार**, **अभ्यास**, **प्रयोजन** — all these have to be kept in mind. **उपपत्ति** also. But keeping everything in mind, what is **शास्त्र**-**तात्पर्यम्** has to be ascertained. And what is **श्रवणम्** here further? It is that **तात्पर्यम्** which leads you to see that **आत्मा** is **जगत्कारणं** **ब्रह्म** **एव**। That kind of a listening is called **श्रवणम्**।

All right — I listened, the **शास्त्र** told. I understand the **शास्त्र** is saying everything is **ब्रह्मन्** and **तत्त्वमसि**। I understand. But how can it be? How can it be? **शङ्कर** himself says — even if ten **शास्त्र**s, a thousand **शास्त्र**s come and say "**अग्नि** is cold to touch," what should you do? You cannot accept the **शास्त्र**; you have to find another meaning for the **शास्त्र**। You cannot say that this part of the **शास्त्र** will be rejected, the rest of it you keep — that is called **अर्धजरतीय** **न्याय**। You cannot have half an egg for hatching and half an egg for eating. Doesn't work that way. Either the whole **शास्त्र** is real or the whole **शास्त्र** is not. You cannot take a piecemeal approach. This is what a lot of times we do — you quote the **शास्त्र** where it suits you, and you forget the **शास्त्र** where it doesn't suit you, which means you become an opportunist even with the **शास्त्र**। No — the whole **शास्त्र** is **प्रमाणम्**। Therefore you understood.

Now the problem is what? I understand what the **शास्त्र** says, I can't agree. If that is so, then what is the next part? **मन्तव्यः**। Then **मन्तव्यः** — so **मननम्** **कर्तव्य**:। That is meant to do what? Remove my doubts, my conclusions which stand against what the **शास्त्र** is revealing. That's the purpose of **मननम्**। And even after I do that — okay, I have no reason by which I can deny what the **शास्त्र** is saying, but I still can't go with it; my orientation is extremely strong, "this much alone I am" — then what? Then **निदिध्यासितव्यः**। So it is one teaching. **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः** is one teaching, and for that the method of understanding is threefold — not three different means, threefold. **श्रवणम्** alone is the **अङ्गी**। **मनन**-**निदिध्यासन** are the **अङ्गाः** — which means **मनन** and **निदिध्यासन** supplement **श्रवणम्**, they don't stand independently. **तात्पर्य**-**निश्चय** is the goal. All right — this is what is **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **श्रोतव्यः**, **मन्तव्यः**, **निदिध्यासितव्यः**।

Let's go back to the next. **तस्मात्** **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः** **दर्शनार्हः**, **दर्शनविषयम्** **आपादयितव्यः**; **श्रोतव्यः** **पूर्वम्** **आचार्यतः** - from **आचार्य**; and **आगमतश्च** - in keeping with the **शास्त्र** ; **पश्चात्** - after that **मन्तव्यः** **तर्कतः** - by logic; **ततो** - after that **निदिध्यासितव्यः** **निश्चयेन** **ध्यातव्यः** - with clear understanding you dwell, you abide in it; **एवं** **हि** **असौ** **दृष्टो** **भवति** - in this way alone it comes to be known; **श्रवण**-**मनन**-**निदिध्यासन**-**साधनैः** **निर्वर्तितैः** - all these must be used as needed not only one; **यदा** **एकत्वम्** **एतानि** **उपगतानि** - when all these three becomes one means of knowledge to you and understand that they are not three different things, **तदा** **सम्यग्दर्शनं** **ब्रह्मैकत्व**-**विषयं** **प्रसीदति** — only then, basically when you have understood **शास्त्र**-**तात्पर्यम्**, removed the objections you have in your mind against it, and you have removed the habitual wrong thinking from your head, then alone there is abidance — not just by listening - **इति** "the **शास्त्र** said.” Then

यत् ब्रह्मक्षत्रादि कर्मनिमित्तं वर्णाश्रमादिलक्षणम् आत्मन्यविद्याध्यारोपितप्रत्ययविषयं क्रियाकारकफलात्मकम् अविद्याप्रत्ययविषयम् — रज्ज्वामिव सर्पप्रत्ययः, तदुपमर्दनार्थमाह;

Now he is introducing the next sentence. What is the next sentence in the **श्रुति**? It is, after **निदिध्यासितव्यः** — **मैत्रेयि** **आत्मनो** **वा** **अरे** **दर्शनेन** **श्रवणेन** **मत्या** **विज्ञानेन** **इदं** **सर्वं** **विदितम्**। What does it say? Hey **मैत्रेयि**, **आत्मनो** **वा** **अरे** **दर्शनेन** — only by seeing clearly what **आत्मा** is. **द्रष्टव्यः** was the **विधि**, was the teaching — it should be seen. Now what has happened after learning? **दर्शनम्** has happened. You are seeing it clearly. You can't miss it, because all you see is that. Therefore, **आत्मनो** **वै** **अरे** **दर्शनेन**। And how do you get that **दर्शनम्**? **श्रवणेन** — by listening. **मत्या** — by doing **मननम्**। **विज्ञानेन** — by doing **निदिध्यासन**। **सर्वं** **विदितम्**

“Hey I came to know that I want to be the pleased self — why are you bringing **सर्वं** **विदितम्** here? What is the correlation? I let the world go to dogs, I don't care, I want to be happy." It doesn't work that way, because you see, all these seem to somehow connect to you. And even if you say "no, no, nothing bothers me," they will bother you — if not now, they'll bother you after five minutes, just wait. So the question is — if there are other things as true as I am, as real as I am, there is no way that I can abide in freedom. If other things are there **द्वैताद्** **भयं** **भवति।** If a second thing is there, it means you are going to be limited — in space you will be limited, in capacity you will be limited; somewhere that fellow will have overriding authority. You cannot have total freedom. Therefore, what is required for you to be always happy — to be the pleased self? Nothing should come between you and the pleased self. If nothing should come, everything should be under my control — that's what we normally think. Nothing is in my control. Therefore then, what should be done? Suppose there is nothing at all that goes against you — if nothing goes against you, then it's there but it's aligned with your likes and dislikes. But what is the fact? They are not aligned. In fact, even if somebody tries to align with my likes and dislikes, my likes and dislikes themselves are constantly changing. What I wanted one year ago is not what I want today. In fact, what I ate two days ago is not what I want today — I'm bored with it, I want something different. You saw a movie, you liked it very much — come on, let's go for it every day! For how many days? You say "enough." Anything you like, you like it only till you like it; then you say enough. And you said "I don't like this at all, I don't like this" — then after some days you developed a liking for it, now you like it. How can anybody satisfy you? Therefore, if there is a second thing, it's impossible to be pleased, to abide in that pleased self. Therefore, what's required?

And moreover, the question is this — even after **मननम्**, a question will come; typically it will be: "But what about all this? But what about **कर्म**?" So many questions come in. What is the place of everything? If everything is myself, why is **जगत्** created? The mind never rests. Therefore he is saying — look, by this what will happen? **सर्वं** **विदितं** **स्यात्**। Hey — I thought you said **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्य**: - **आत्मा** should be known? By knowing **आत्मा**, suppose you know — I ask you, what is this? This is a mouse. Good. Now, by that, do you know what this is (now Swamiji is showing a pen)? Not because of that (because of mouse). You may know what this is (pen), but not because you know what that is (mouse). By knowing one thing, how can other things be known? By knowing one thing you know one thing. Therefore, if you know **आत्मा**, what should you know? You should know **आत्मा**। Therefore, if by knowing **आत्मा** everything has to become known, what does it mean? **आत्मा** is everything. See how it is being said subtly — the **महावाक्य** is sitting there. **आत्मनो** **दर्शनेन** **सर्वं** **विदितं** **स्यात्** — **कथम्**? Because **सर्वं** **खलु** **आत्मा** **एव**। That is what is being said.

So he is saying — look, now the **भाष्यकार** wants to introduce this sentence. Before the sentence comes, what does he do? He says:

यत् ब्रह्म-क्षत्रादि-कर्म-निमित्तं वर्णाश्रमादि-लक्षणम् आत्मनि अविद्या-अध्यारोपित-प्रत्यय-विषयं क्रिया-कारक-फलात्मकम् अविद्या-प्रत्यय-विषयम् — रज्ज्वाम् इव सर्प-प्रत्ययः, तदुपमर्दनार्थम् आह।

See, what are all these now? What is **समुपचक्रमे** ? **चक्रमे** — not **चक्र**; **चक्रमे** is from the **कृ** - **धातु** - **चकार** like that **चक्रमे** - **आत्मनेपद** | **समुपचक्रमे** — so it begins. What does it begin? **वक्तुम्** — it begins to talk. What does it begin to talk? **न** **वा** **अरे** **जायायाः** **कामाय** **जाया** **प्रिया** **भवति** — **न** **वा** **अरे** **पत्युः** **कामाय** **पतिः** **प्रियो** **भवति** — it begins like that. **न** **वा** **अरे** — that's the quotation. Why did it begin this? **वैराग्यहेतून्** — to tell you what is **वैराग्य**-**हेतु**: the understanding that nobody loves anything for that object's sake, or for that relationship's sake. Only by understanding that, you get **वैराग्य** — not by staying with the relationship or doing things. Doing is necessary to afford it, but knowledge is very important. Therefore he begins.

Then he makes another statement:

यस्मिन्नेव परा सक्तिस्तस्मिन्नेव निरादरम्। कर्म कृत्वा परं यत्नादन्यस्यार्थयते फलम्॥ ७४ ॥

When you do a **कर्म**, what is your commitment to, normally? Anyone's commitment when you undertake an action — **परासक्ति**. Why do you do a **कर्म**? What is the purpose of a **क्रिया**? **क्रिया**'s purpose is what you accomplish from the **क्रिया**. What is it you accomplish? **कर्म**. Not **क्रिया** — **कर्म**. What is **कर्म**? What is the definition of **कर्म** as per **पाणिनि**? It is **कर्तुः** **ईप्सिततमं** **कर्म**. What is most desired to be attained through an action is called the object of the **कर्म**, of the **क्रिया**. That is **कर्म**. So, **यस्मिन्नेव** **परा** **सक्तिः** — when you do a **कर्म**, what is your commitment? To attain that object of that **क्रिया**. So **कर्मफल** is your commitment. **यस्मिन्नेव** **परा** **सक्तिः** — that **कर्मफल** to which you are committed. **तस्मिन्नेव** **निरादरम्** — with respect to that same **कर्मफल**, you become what? You disregard the **कर्मफल**. **कर्म** you do, but without the **आसक्ति** for **कर्मफल**. **यस्मिन्नेव** **आसक्तिः** — **प्राक्**, as a pure **कर्मी**, doing **कर्म**s — **तस्मिन्नेव**, in that same **कर्म**, in that same **कर्मफल**, **निरादरम्** — disregard for **कर्मफल**. Not that you don't care. No idiot starts a **कर्म** without caring for **कर्मफल**. Therefore **प्रयोजनमनुद्दिश्य** **न** **मन्दोऽपि** **प्रवर्तते** **इति** **भाष्यकार**, you know, without keeping an end in view, even an idiot will not begin something. The fellow who tells somebody — somebody talks, "Do action without caring for the results, don't do action for results" — you tell him: "Are you listening so that I understand, or are you listening because you just want to talk? You are talking because you want somebody to hear you and come to your ashrama. You publish a book with that, so that you can sell so many copies. You publish a thousand copies, you are hoping to sell a thousand copies. Do you have **कर्मफल** in view or not?" No idiot can do anything without **कर्मफल** in view. **प्रयोजनमनुद्दिश्य** **न** **मन्दोऽपि** **प्रवर्तते**

Then what is **कर्मयोग**? Then what is the right attitude to **कर्म**? Keeping an end in view, but allowing the result to unfold. What do you mean, allow the result to unfold? I have a goal — **कर्म** again to realign the outcome. It's like — you want to send a satellite to space, and you want it to be in geosynchronous orbit; it has to be. You cannot make it go straight to geosynchronous orbit. You have what are called small motors, I believe. And you fire a motor in a particular direction, then the thrust will be in the opposite direction — Newton's third law. You align how you fire the motors to realign your satellite to become geosynchronous. So, in fact, you send a drone, you fire a missile — what do you do? They are all guided. What is guided? They are guided based on GPS. And the fellow, the enemy, will try to distract it by putting something out here and there. You have to prevent a distraction, and again guide yourself. Basically, you will have a tendency to move off course; you have to again come back to course. That coming back to course is a fresh action — not resisting the old action. "Ayyo, I put the satellite in the wrong orbit." Ayyo, if you do, nothing will happen. Okay? You should know what motor to fire — that will push it in the right direction. Now you fired a motor, you want to make it go a little west; it went too far west; then you fire again, go and make it go east. This is the way **कर्म** has to work. So he says — **यस्मिन्नेव** **परा** **सक्तिः**, **तस्मिन्नेव** **निरादरम्**, **कर्म** **कृत्वा** **परं** **यत्नात्** — not without doing **कर्म**; **कर्म** **कृत्वा**, **यत्नात्**, with effort — **अन्यस्य** **अर्थयते** **फलम्** — **ज्ञानस्य** **फलम्** **अर्थयते**. You are doing **कर्म** for **ज्ञान**-**फलम्** ultimately. Why? Because you are doing **कर्म** for **चित्तशुद्धि**.

Amazing how **सुरेश्वर** begins here. He is talking **अद्वैत**, but he makes sure you don't forget the **अधिकारी**. Because the mantra did not forget the **अधिकारी**. The mantra wants the **अधिकारी** to have, to be one who has got **वैराग्यम्**. And **वैराग्यम्** cannot come without **ज्ञानम्**. But that **ज्ञानम्** does not get solidified unless **अन्तःकरण**-**शुद्धि** happens. And **अन्तःकरण**-**शुद्धि** doesn't happen unless you do **कर्म** without being tied to **कर्मफल**. All that is sitting here. Then a question is visualized — okay? "How by I doing **कर्म** will I get **वैराग्यम्**. **कर्म** is going to give **कर्मफल**, it is going to bind me."

किमिक्षो रसमाप्नोति यत्नान्निष्पीडयन्न ना॥ ७५ ॥

**सुरेश्वर** says — that **ना** in the end is **नरः**. Okay. **ना**, **नरौ**, **नर**: is **नृ** that is the **प्रातिपदिक**— a human being. **किम्** **इक्षोः** **रसम्** **आप्नोति** **यत्नात्** **निष्पीडयन्** **न** — suppose you have a sugar cane. There is an effort. Not today's modern cane crushers — you go to the cane crushers and they say, "Come on, give me a sugar cane juice." That's not the example. You have access to a clean sugar cane — **पोङ्गल** day, you got one sugar cane with you. Now what do you want? You have to eat it. How will you eat? You will do this — okay, and it's not easy. Many of us now — you try, we can't do, because teeth are incapable of doing it, perhaps. But you have to do. And even when you do, the tongue also gets, you know, cut up. You do. And finally, when you bite that, you can't eat it — okay, you have to spit out. When you bite it, there is pain involved. But don't you get the sweet, sweet juice coming from it? Like that, you do **कर्म** with efforts, and the dew of **ज्ञान** will come to you. It is **सुरेश्वर** quietly who says that here.

शीतमल्पं सुखं ग्रीष्मे तापोऽल्पः शिशिरे सुखम्। तदेव दुःखकृद्भूरि तद्धेत्वोरनवस्थितेः॥ ७७ ॥

**शीतमल्पं** **सुखं** **ग्रीष्मे** - **ग्रीष्म** is what? The weather, summer. It's called **ग्रीष्म**. **ग्रीष्म**-**ऋतु**. So, **शीतम्** **अल्पं** **सुखं** **ग्रीष्मे** — in summer, you know, you go to some place. Banda — 48 degrees Celsius apparently now, the temperature, the hottest in India. 48 degrees Celsius. In that place, suppose you get one mild cool breeze — how will it feel? **सुखम्**. **शीतम्** **अल्पं** Even a little cool, cold breeze. When? In summer. But what happens in winter? **तापः** **अल्पः** **शिशिरे** **सुखम्** - **शिशिर** is winter. **शिशिरवसन्तौ** **पुनरायातः** - **भज** **गोविन्दम्** - if you remember. So, **शिशिरे**, in winter **तापः** **अल्पः** **सुखम्**— a little heat is very pleasant. You get one heater and you put your hand in it, when you are freezing like this — it's so nice, heat, very nice. **तदेव** **दुःखकृद्भूरि** The same heat — I will give it to you in 48 degrees Celsius, I will give you a heater in your room — what will happen? Or when you are trembling in Rishikesh, I will put an air conditioner for you, cooler — what will happen? It will become **दुःख**. Therefore, what he is saying — **तद्धेत्वोः** **अनवस्थितेः** — **तस्य** **सुखस्य** **हेतोः** **अनवस्थितेः** — **अनवस्थिति**, it is not fixed there; **अवस्थितिः** **हि** **नास्ति**. The **सुख**-**हेतु** — you cannot have this kind of understanding is impossible — that heat is **सुख**-**हेतु**, cold is **सुख**-**हेतु**, food is **सुख**-**हेतु**, relationship is **सुख**-**हेतु**, money is **सुख**-**हेतु**. Anything — if you say **सुख**-**हेतु**, he is saying there is no such thing; there is no such **अवस्था**. **तद्धेत्वोः** **अनवस्थितेः** | He has finished the topic of, you know, **कर्मयोग**; now he is entering what this section is talking about — why **पतिः** **कामाय** **पतिः** **प्रियो** **भवति**, **आत्मनस्तु** **कामाय** **पतिः** **प्रियो** **भवति**? Why? Because any **हेतु** is **अनवस्थित**. Anything which you think will make you pleased, that **हेतु** is not fixed — just like in summer you like something cold, in winter you like heat. In that way, what makes you pleased depends on a lot of factors. But what is unchanging? You want to be pleased. The pleased you is unchanging in and through all of them. That's the commitment to show here, okay?

So then he says:

शब्दोऽस्ति न प्रियः कश्चिदप्रियो वा स्वतस्तथा। रक्तद्विष्टमनो हेतुः शब्दाद्यर्थे प्रियाप्रिये॥ ७८ ॥

**शब्दः** **अस्ति** **न** **प्रियः** **कश्चित्** — no sound is dear to you by itself. **अप्रियो** **वा** **स्वतः** **तथा** — and no sound is **अप्रिय** by itself. Now the ear is being considered. What sound — is there a sound which is good? Is there a sound which is dear? You go north in India — **शहनाई** is considered to be a **मङ्गल**-**वाद्यम्**. So if there is a wedding, what will you hear? You will hear the **शहनाई**. And for somebody in the south, when they hear it, they will say, "What is this mourning?" — they feel like it. What is our **मङ्गल**-**वाद्यम्**? You should have **नादस्वरम्**. No mic is needed; you can listen to it in the whole village — big pipe, and the **तविल्** which goes with that — that sound you can hear from a mile, probably. I don't know how much strength they must have to play these instruments. Not ordinary. Somebody came for a concert and Pūjya Swami is there. The mic was being put. They said, "Please remove the mic. The mic is not needed in a small hall with that." **नादस्वरम्** is like that. But I am saying — is **शहनाई** a **अप्रिय**? Is **नादस्वरम्** **प्रिय**? Which is **प्रिय**? Which is **अप्रिय**? Which is **प्रिय**-**हेतु**, which is **अप्रिय**-**हेतु**? Showing **अनवस्थित**, **अनवस्थिति**. So he says, **रक्तद्विष्टमनो** **हेतुः** — **मनः** **एव** **हेतुः**. **रक्त**-**द्विष्ट** — **रक्त** is what? Attached. **द्विष्ट** is what? Hate. **राग**-**द्वेष**. **रक्त**-**द्विष्ट**-**मनो** **हेतुः** — means what? **राग**-**द्वेष**-**युक्तं** **मनः** **हेतुः**. For what? **शब्दाद्यर्थे** **प्रियाप्रिये** — "this is dear to me, this is not dear to me," is all because of the mind's likes and dislikes, which you want to identify with and say, "It's my likes and dislikes."

स्वतोऽखिलोऽप्रियोऽनात्मा प्रत्यङ्मोहैकहेतुतः। प्रत्यगाह्लादकारित्वादप्रियोऽपि प्रियो मतः॥ ७९ ॥

**स्वतः** **अखिलः** **अप्रियः** **अनात्मा** — by itself, without you backing it with your wrong thinking — **स्वतः**, by itself, anything — **अखिलः**, without any exception; **अखिल** is "remaining," **अखिल** is without exception — **स्वतः** **अखिलः** **अप्रियः**; **किम्** **अप्रियः**? **अनात्मा** **अप्रियः**. **स्वतः** **अखिलः** **अनात्मा** **अप्रियः** — anything other than you, by itself, has no way to become dear to you. It is **अप्रिय**. Okay? **प्रत्यङ्मोहैकहेतुतः**. Let's put it this way — any **अनात्मा** which is not dear to you, why is it not dear to you? **प्रत्यङ्मोहैकहेतुतः**. **प्रत्यक्** means inner. Inner — you keep going inner. What does inner mean? Not outer. What is outer? What can be objectified. Anything that can be objectified is outer. What is innermost? What can never be objectified. See, "inner" can give you a spatial feeling — you will try to find **आत्मा** inside now. "So there is this body; in the body there is one heart; in that heart there is a little space; in that space is **आत्मा**." Sounds very nice. **हृदय**-**देह**-**मध्ये**, **हृदय**-**कोहर**-**मध्ये**, **केवलम्** **अहम्** **अहम्** **इति** — this is **उपासना**-**अर्थे**, **हृदयं** **स्थानम्** — that is not being questioned; but if you think **आत्मा** is confined to a space in your heart, you are off. So he says, **प्रत्यक्** means **आत्मा**. **प्रत्यङ्मोहैकहेतुतः** — on account of not knowing who you are, something is not dear to you, something is dear to you. **प्रत्यगाह्लादकारित्वात्** — and this **आत्मा**, something makes it happy **आह्लादकारि**. **आह्लाद** is happiness — **आह्लाद** all the way, **आह्लादकारी**. **प्रत्यगाह्लादकारी** means what pleases me; on account of pleasing me — **प्रत्यगाह्लादकारित्वात्** **अप्रियोऽपि** **प्रियो** **मतः**. By itself, nothing is really **प्रिय**; nothing which is **अनात्मा** is **प्रिय**, because it is not.

See, what is **अनात्मा**? Another thing you can think of, right? **अनात्मा** is "not us," basically. But what is it that you care for? Do you care for something which is totally unconnected to you? A thing which is totally unconnected to you can neither be a source of pleasure nor a source of pain — nothing, it's zero. Therefore, what bothers you has become — what? — first connected to **आत्मा** in some way, what you take yourself to be. That is called **आत्म**-**शेष**. **शेष** means connected. How? **शेषे** **षष्ठी**, not **कारक**-**सम्बन्ध**. See grammar, how it connects — it's called **शेष**. So **आत्म**-**शेष** means what? Not something other than **आत्मा**. **आत्म**-**शेष** means what is connected to you. What is unconnected to you cannot be pleasing or displeasing. What's connected to you — if you say it makes me pleased, it becomes dear to you; and if you say it doesn't please me, it becomes undear to you. Therefore, what is the **हेतु**? Who is saying it's dear or undear? That is **रक्त**-**द्विष्ट**-**मनः**. Therefore, this is what is driving your **प्रिय**-**अप्रिय**. **आत्मा** in itself is neither **प्रिय** nor **अप्रिय**. See, I am changing it slightly — I am saying **आत्मा** itself is neither **प्रिय** nor **अप्रिय**. I can say that way also. That's important here. **प्रत्यगाह्लादकारित्वात्** **अप्रियोऽपि** **प्रियो** **मतः** - **अप्रियोऽपि** **प्रियायते** .
