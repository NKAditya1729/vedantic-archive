We just entered the sixth **mantra**, and the fourth **brāhmaṇa** of the second **adhyāya** — **maitreyī** **brāhmaṇa**. So the fifth **mantra** was a **mantra** which said — what we are really seeking, what is really dear to us is what we are seeking. And what's really dear to us is ourselves. So everything else is loved is a derived love. It's not a love for the thing per se, but because somehow it makes me feel good. So all that I really love is the pleased me — the me without any limitations, any agenda. And that me seems to come out in the presence of, or in the absence of, something. And I have attributed my being comfortable to those situations or objects or relationships, and I'm insecure without them. And therefore I have to discern the fact that what I really love myself is a secure, comfortable me which does not depend on anything else. And that me is to be discovered, is to be known.
