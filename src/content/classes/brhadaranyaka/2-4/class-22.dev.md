Even as your bangle is melting away — the gold is untouched. Even as everything is changing, there is an unchanging reality. So — **सर्वदा** **अव्यभिचारि** — that which never goes away. **व्यभिचार्यर्थबुद्धिषु** — where do you spot it? In the **बुद्धि** alone. Which **बुद्धि**?
