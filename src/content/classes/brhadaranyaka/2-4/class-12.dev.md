And once, if that is done, still there is a habitual problem, then **निदिध्यासन** has to be done. **निदिध्यासन** can be **शब्दानुविद्ध**, **दृश्यानुविद्ध**। So all this — till when should you do? Till you don't need to do anything more.
Therefore, **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः**। How? **श्रोतव्यः**, **मन्तव्यः**, **निदिध्यासितव्यः**। What will happen if I do that? I will know **आत्मा**। What about **अनात्मा**? **अनात्मा** will go away. Because my thinking is, **आत्मा** is different. So then, what does it say? So you can see **एवं** **ह्यसौ** **दृष्टो** **भवति** **श्रवण-मनन-निदिध्यासन-साधनैर्निर्वर्तितैः** । **यदा** **एकत्वमेतान्युपगतानि**, **तदा** **सम्यग्दर्शनं**—what is that? **ब्रह्मैकत्व-विषयं**. You said **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **श्रोतव्यः** — you should say — you said **आत्मा** is to be understood — **आत्मा** **वा** **अरे** **द्रष्टव्यः**। Correct? **आत्मा** is to be known. For that you should do **श्रवणम्**, **मननम्**, **निदिध्यासनम्**। Then suddenly, what is the outcome of that knowledge? **सम्यग्दर्शनम्**। Proper understanding of what? Of **आत्मा**। Where did this come suddenly? **ब्रह्मैकत्व-विषयं** **प्रसीदति** — from where did you get this **ब्रह्मन्** suddenly? Now, we were talking of you, **आत्मा**। What does it mean? If by understanding **आत्मा** you know **ब्रह्मन्**, what does it mean? **ब्रह्मन्** is **आत्मा**, okay. There is no **ब्रह्मन्** other than **आत्मा**। So **ब्रह्मत्व-विषय**, **ब्रह्म** **एकत्वम्**। **ब्रह्मैकत्व** means what? **ऐक्यम्**। **आत्म-ब्रह्मणोः** **ऐक्यम्**। **ब्रह्मैकत्व**। Or you can see **ब्रह्मैव** **इदं** **सर्वम्** — **एकत्वम्** — there is only **ब्रह्मन्**। **ब्रह्मैकत्व** can be looked at both ways. Basically, in one I am saying only **महावाक्य**, in the other one I am saying there is nothing but **ब्रह्मन्** — same story. **ब्रह्मैकत्व-विषयं** **प्रसीदति**। **प्रसीदति** means — you know, that clarity comes, that what is here is only one **ब्रह्मात्मा**। And this clarity, **भाष्यकार** says, will only come when all these three are adequately applied, used as needed. And further what? **न** **अन्यथा** **श्रवणमात्रेण** — and not otherwise, only by doing **वेदान्त** **श्रवणम्**, just by listening. "Swami, listening is not enough?" Maybe enough. But listening is an inquiring mind. Listening with **तात्पर्यम्**। Listening with **श्रद्धा**। Listening by an **अधिकारी**। All that is included in — but even with all that, just listening may not suffice. I won't say "will not suffice", "may not suffice." Generally will not suffice. One exception in a billion may be there. Therefore, **न** **अन्यथा** **श्रवणमात्रेण**।
Then what will happen if you gain that? Then what if you understand that? **हे** **मैत्रेयि**, **आत्मनः** **वा** **अरे** **दर्शनेन** **श्रवणेन** **मत्या** **विज्ञानेन** **इदं** **सर्वं** **विदितम्** — **इदं** **सर्वं** **विदितम्** — the whole world becomes known to you as yourself. So that's what **भाष्यकार** will say, that you know that — **आत्मनि** **दृष्टे** **श्रुते** **मते** **विज्ञाते** **इदं** **सर्वं** **विदितम्**। So, what does that mean? By saying that last sentence in the **मन्त्र**, it means that you will see that everything else that you count, this whole **द्वैत** **प्रपञ्च**, you will see is sublated, is swallowed in the understanding of this **ब्रह्मात्मा**। And once that happens, there is no more anything to be done, because there is no second thing to be known. There is no second thing for you to do any action either. There is no **कर्म** possible, no **ज्ञान** possible, nothing is needed, because what is, is only one. That's the teaching. We'll stop there.
