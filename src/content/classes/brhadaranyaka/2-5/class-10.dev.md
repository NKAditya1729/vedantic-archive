    <p class="src-bhashyam"><span class="src-pratika">तदेतदृषिः</span> — <span class="src-pratika">तदेतद्</span> कर्म, <span class="src-pratika">ऋषिः</span> मन्त्रः, <span class="src-pratika">पश्यन्</span> उपलभमानः, <span class="src-pratika">अवोचत्</span> उक्तवान् ; कथम् ? <span class="src-pratika">तत्</span> दंस इति व्यवहितेन सम्बन्धः, <span class="src-pratika">दंस</span> इति कर्मणो नामधेयम् ; तच्च दंसः किंविशिष्टम् ? <span class="src-pratika">उग्रं</span> क्रूरम् , <span class="src-pratika">वां</span> युवयोः, हे <span class="src-pratika">नरा</span> नराकारावश्विनौ ; तच्च कर्म किं निमित्तम् ? <span class="src-pratika">सनये</span> लाभाय ; लाभलुब्धो हि लोकेऽपि क्रूरं कर्म आचरति, तथैव एतावुपलभ्येते यथा लोके ; तत् <span class="src-pratika">आविः</span> प्रकाशं <span class="src-pratika">कृणोमि</span> करोमि, यत् रहसि भवद्भ्यां कृतम् ; किमिवेत्युच्यते — <span class="src-pratika">तन्यतुः</span> पर्जन्यः, <span class="src-pratika">न</span> इव ; नकारस्तु उपरिष्टादुपचार उपमार्थीयो वेदे, न प्रतिषेधार्थः — यथा ‘अश्वं न’ (ऋ. सं. १ । ६ । २४ । १) अश्वमिवेति यद्वत् ; तन्यतुरिव <span class="src-pratika">वृष्टिं</span> यथा पर्जन्यो वृष्टिं प्रकाशयति स्तनयित्न्वादिशब्दैः, तद्वत् अहं युवयोः क्रूरं कर्म आविष्कृणोमीति सम्बन्धः ।</p>

    <p class="src-bhashyam"><span class="src-pratika">इदं वै तन्मध्वि</span>त्यादि पूर्ववत् मन्त्रान्तरप्रदर्शनार्थम् । तथा अन्यो मन्त्रः तामेव आख्यायिकामनुसरति स्म । आथर्वणो दध्यङ्नाम — आथर्वणोऽन्यो विद्यत इत्यतो विशिनष्टि — दध्यङ्नाम आथर्वणः, तस्मै <span class="src-pratika">दधीचे</span> <span class="src-pratika">आथर्वणाय</span>, हे <span class="src-pratika">अश्विनावि</span>ति मन्त्रदृशो वचनम् ; <span class="src-pratika">अश्व्यम्</span> अश्वस्य स्वभूतम् , <span class="src-pratika">शिरः</span>, ब्राह्मणस्य शिरसि च्छिन्ने अश्वस्य शिरश्छित्त्वा ईदृशमतिक्रूरं कर्म कृत्वा अश्व्यं शिरः ब्राह्मणं <span class="src-pratika">प्रति</span> <span class="src-pratika">ऐरयतं</span> गमितवन्तौ, युवाम् ; <span class="src-pratika">स</span> च आथर्वणः <span class="src-pratika">वां</span> युवाभ्याम् तन्म<span class="src-pratika">धु</span> <span class="src-pratika">प्रवोचत्</span> , यत्पूर्वं प्रतिज्ञातम् — वक्ष्यामीति । स किमर्थमेवं जीवितसन्देहमारुह्य प्रवोचदित्युच्यते — <span class="src-pratika">ऋतायन्</span> यत्पूर्वं प्रतिज्ञातं सत्यं तत्परिपालयितुमिच्छन् ; जीवितादपि हि सत्यधर्मपरिपालना गुरुतरेत्येतस्य लिङ्गमेतत् । किं तन्मधु प्रवोचदित्युच्यते — <span class="src-pratika">त्वाष्ट्रम्</span> , त्वष्टा आदित्यः, तस्य सम्बन्धि — यज्ञस्य शिरश्छिन्नं त्वष्ट्रा अभवत् , तत्प्रतिसन्धानार्थं प्रवर्ग्यं कर्म, तत्र प्रवर्ग्यकर्माङ्गभूतं यद्विज्ञानं तत् त्वाष्ट्रं मधु — यत्तस्य चिरश्छेदनप्रतिसन्धानादिविषयं दर्शनं तत् त्वाष्ट्रं यन्मधु ; हे <span class="src-pratika">दस्रौ</span> दस्राविति परबलानामुपक्षपयितारौ शत्रूणां हिंसितारौ ; अपि च न केवलं त्वाष्ट्रमेव मधु कर्मसम्बन्धि युवाभ्यामवोचत् ; अपि च <span class="src-pratika">कक्ष्यं</span> गोप्यं रहस्यं परमात्मसम्बन्धि यद्विज्ञानं मधु मधुब्राह्मणेनोक्तं अध्यायद्वयप्रकाशितम् , तच्च वां युवाभ्यां प्रवोचदित्यनुवर्तते ॥</p>

ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

All right. We are on the story time. So the story is — the story is said to praise **ब्रह्मविद्या**. How it praise **ब्रह्मविद्या** we have to figure it out, but it is supposed to be said to praise **ब्रह्मविद्या**, because of the effort involved and the dedication of the teacher and the students. With so much at stake — life is at stake; head is going to be transplanted out and transplanted in. I mean, it's not ordinary. This kind of thing, who would undertake? That's the praise there. So that story will go on for some time now, and then the last two mantras are going to talk of the **वेदान्तशास्त्र**.

Because in that story also, this **दध्यङ्** **ऋषि** first apparently tells about a **चिकित्सा**, this plastic surgery he talks about there. So there is some **उपासना** or something for that, in some other context in the **श्रुति** that he mentions; that is called **प्रवर्ग्य** **कर्म**. And then he talks about **ब्रह्मविद्या**. So in this story now, 2.5.16 and 17 are just the story part, and mentioning that he taught this thing there; and then 18 and 19 are: what did he say about **ब्रह्मविद्या**? That's the arrangement. Okay.

So we'll look at the story. And in the story you can see — it's **भाष्यम्**-wise, it's more difficult in a way, because you don't know where to put what words. They go all over, and it's all **श्रुति** words, so you don't understand what they mean. That's the way the story part is. But our interest is not in the story. Our interest is in — this story has to say something, you know, such is so important: **ब्रह्मविद्या**, **मधुविद्या**. That is what our interest is. Okay. So with that we will get into the text. One second. Okay.

All right, so what we were looking at last time — let's go there. We were looking at 2.5.16. So we had seen this much: that this is for what. Let's look at the mantra briefly. **इदं** **वै** **तन्मधु** **दध्यङ्ङाथर्वणोऽश्विभ्यामुवाच** । This **इदं** **वै** is doing a recollection of what was said in some other place. This was taught by **आथर्वण** **ऋषि**, whose name is — **आथर्वण** is a **तद्धित**: **अथर्वस्य** **पुत्रः**, or **अथर्वगोत्रोद्भवः**, something like that. So **आथर्वण** is a generic name. You find this quite often in the Veda. Gautama: **गौतम** is **गोतमगोत्रोद्भवः**. Or **भारद्वाज**, **वासिष्ठ** — you use these words. So then it doesn't tell you much about who it is, because there could be a hundred people in that family, in that lineage. Therefore one name: **दध्यङ्**. **दध्यङ्** is the name. This **दध्यङ्** **आथर्वणः** — **आथर्वण** by the name **दध्यङ्**. He taught this **मधुविद्या** to whom? To the **Aśvinī** **Kumāras**. Okay.

**तदेतदृषिः** **पश्यन्नवोचत्** । So this **ऋषि**, having seen that — **एतत्** **कर्म** — **अवोचत्**, he said: **तद्वां** **नरा** **सनये** **दंस** **उग्रमाविष्कृणोमि**. So none of these words you can understand clearly, what it means. **तत्**, you know. Okay, do you know what is **वां** **नरा** **सनये** **दंस**? All these seem to be a little off. So that's how the words are here. We'll look at the **भाष्य** as we go. But what he's saying is this: you know, you both **Aśvinī** **Kumāras**, you both did a lot of thing out of greed. Out of greed for **ब्रह्मविद्या** you guys took off the neck of your teacher, took off the neck of a horse. This is what you did. I am going to tell everybody about what all you did. Does this look like praise? Yes, it is praise. Okay, **भाष्यकार** will show what he has to say about it.

So **उग्रम्** — **उग्रं** **कर्म** **आविष्करोमि**. I will — I will publish this. What you did very secretly, I will make this public. I am going to put it on Facebook now. Okay. So like what? **तन्यतुर्न** **वृष्टिम्**. **न** here is **इव**. See, **न** does not mean "not"; **न** here means "like". **तन्यतु** is thunder; **वृष्टि** is rain. So if it just rains, you may not know it rained. You may not know it rained. What does thunder do? It advertises the rain. That's the example, okay. It announces the rain loudly. You're sleeping in the night; you wake up because you hear this thunder, huge thunderclaps. Then you know what? It rained in the night; it rained heavily. Why? Thunder announced it. You know, it woke you up and told you it's raining. In the same way, I am going to thunder out what you did; all your messy action I am going to talk about. Okay? So — and you know, how you took off his head and you put the head of a horse on him, I am going to tell. By telling this, I am going to tell the world what kind of an effort you went into to get **ब्रह्मविद्य**, and what kind of an effort a teacher went into to teach **ब्रह्मविद्य**. Therefore it is the **स्तुति**. Okay, that's the idea here.

So now — so we had seen, I think, till here: **अश्विभ्यामुवाच** । **तदेतदृषिः**. **तदेतदृषिः**. So this part, **तदेतदृषिः**: **तत्** **एतत्**. See, **तत्** — he didn't gloss. **तद्** **ऋषिः**, right? **तत्** **एतत्**. What is **एतत्**?

**तदेतदृषिः** — **तदेतद्** **कर्म**, **ऋषिः** **मन्त्रः** — **एतत्** **कर्म**. **ऋषिः**. So now, this word **ऋषिः**. Normally a **ऋषि** sees a mantra. I could say **ऋषिः** **मन्त्रम्** **अपश्यत्**, or **मन्त्रान्** **अपश्यत्**. But here the case is **मन्त्रः**, one-one; it is **समानाधिकरण** with **ऋषिः**. Therefore **ऋषिः** is equal to **मन्त्रः**. So last time I had said that doesn't seem right; then I found it is a **पाठभेद**. So it could mean **मन्त्र**; in another **पाठभेद** it says — or it is a **मन्त्रद्रष्टा**. So here the **Upaniṣad** is presenting it, right? Therefore this **Upaniṣad** mantra itself can be considered as **ऋषि**, in a way. That's why the word **ऋषिः**, **मन्त्रः**. Otherwise it says **मन्त्रद्रष्टा**. Okay? **मन्त्रः** **पश्यन्** — seeing. Seeing what? The **कर्म** which these people did, **उग्रकर्म**.

**पश्यन्** **उपलभमानः**, **अवोचत्** **उक्तवान्** — **पश्यन्** **उपलभमानः** means gaining, knowing, seeing, obtaining. **अवोचत्**, **अवोचत्** **उक्तवान्** — he said. Okay. What did he say? How did he say?

**कथम्** ? **तत्** **दंस** **इति** **व्यवहितेन** **सम्बन्धः** — so now you see, **तत्** **दंस**: what is **दंस** business here now? If you look in the mantra, you will find it is saying what? It is saying **तदेतदृषिः** **पश्यन्नवोचत्** । **तद्वां** **नरा** **सनये** **दंस** — do you see that first line? **तद्वां** **नरा** **सनये** **दंस**. Now the **तत्** is actually referring to **दंसः**; it is not referring to **वाम्**. **तत्** is singular. Okay? So this **तत्** refers to **दंसः**. How — how do you know? **भाष्यकार** says that. Okay? That is why **भाष्यकार** says here, what? **तत्** **दंस** **इति** **व्यवहितेन** **सम्बन्धः**. So here this **दंस** is not really the **प्रतीक**. This **दंस** is just saying **तत्** **दंस** **इति** — that **तत्** is connecting to **दंस**; that's all it's trying to say. It is not wrong; it is part of the mantra only. But here it's glossing over **तत्** and telling you — **तत्**, you know the meaning already: **तत्** means "that". What is that referring to? It is referring to **दंस**; it is not referring to **वाम्** etcetera.

**व्यवहित** means there is separation in the words. **व्यवहितेन** **सम्बन्धः** means the words are not in prose order directly; you have to now move the words around to connect to the right place. Therefore he is saying **तत्** **दंस** **इति** **व्यवहितेन** **सम्बन्धः**. **तत्** **दंस**. So it should have said **तद्दंस**, but it said what? **तद्वाम्**. That's what the mantra said.
