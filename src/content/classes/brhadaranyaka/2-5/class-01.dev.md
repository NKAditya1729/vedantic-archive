ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

Okay. We looked at **मैत्रेयीविद्या**. **मैत्रेयीविद्या** is the **विद्या** taught to **मैत्रेयी**. Before that, we saw what? **अजातशत्रुविद्या**, taught by **अजातशत्रु** to **दृप्तबालाकि**. What is being taught? **ब्रह्मविद्या**. So job is over. No, there is still some more coming. Now what is coming up? It is called **मधुब्राह्मण** or **मधुविद्या**.

So with names, you cannot be sure why they were called what they are called. **मैत्रेयीविद्या** was because it was taught to **मैत्रेयी**, so this must be taught to **मधु**. No, it is not like that. Neither is **मधु** the teacher here, nor is the first word of the **ब्राह्मण** **मधु**. There is just a convention; some way a name gets coined for a thing. So you cannot conclude too many things by a name. But what is starting now is the fifth **ब्राह्मण** of the **द्वितीय** **अध्याय**, and this **ब्राह्मण** generally is called **मधुब्राह्मण**, and the teaching is sometimes referred to as **मधुविद्या**.

So what does **मधु** mean? We will see. **मधु** has, depending on situations, different meanings at times. If you hear the word **मधु**, **रूढ्या**, what meaning do you get in your head? **मधु** is honey. **मधु** means honey. Sweet. What is sweet usually for us? Honey is sweet, of course. What is sweet? **कर्मफलम्**. So sometimes the word **मधु** is used for **कर्मफलम्**. Here the word **मधु** will be used in a way of meaning it is a **कार्य**, an effect.

So what we have seen in both **अजातशत्रुब्राह्मण** and especially in **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**, it was predominantly a **त्वम्पदविचार**. **तत्पद** we hardly saw. In **अजातशत्रुब्राह्मण** we did see the **सृष्टि** in a way, because we had so many **उपासनाः** being given first by **बालाकि**. And after that **अजातशत्रु** came and used the **अवस्थात्रय** to some extent, and finally came to **नेति** **नेति** and showed us what **ब्रह्मन्** really is, that it has to be **प्राणस्य** **प्राणः**.

So we came from the first chapter, where it said **आत्मेत्येवोपासीत**, 1.4.7. Then we saw the **अविद्यासूत्र**, 1.4.10. Then we also saw, in the same **मन्त्र** before that, we had seen what? **अहं** **ब्रह्मास्मि**. Then after that came 1.5. It was called **सप्तान्नब्राह्मण**. A lot of **सृष्टि**-related things came there. And then it was said, all things we said in **संसार** has been said in the first **अध्याय**. 1.6 was a summary of that. And in 1.6 what did we see? We just saw there are just three things. The whole **जगत्** can be put as three things: **नाम**, **रूप**, **क्रिया**. This much is the truth that we know. But that is not the whole truth. Its **सत्यम्** is **भगवान्**, **प्राणः**. **प्राणा** **वै** **सत्यम्**. Then **प्राणस्य** **सत्यम्** **किम्**? That brought us to the second chapter.

Then in the second chapter we had **बालाकि** coming and saying, “I will teach you. **ब्रह्म** **ते** **ब्रवाणि**.” And **अजातशत्रु** said, “Oh, just for saying that, here are the thousand cows.” And then **बालाकि** went on and on, and at every point **अजातशत्रु** said, “That I already know. Tell me something more.” Finally he had nothing more to say. “Is that all you know? **एतावन्नु**? Is that all you know?” “Yes, that is all I know.” “All right, that is not all.” “Then I am your student. Please teach me.” Then **अजातशत्रु** said, “Okay, you will not become my student, but I will teach you anyway.” Because of the **वर्णभेद**. How can I be your teacher? Therefore he said, “I will not take away that respect. We will keep the **मर्यादा**, but I will make you understand what is **ब्रह्म**.” That is the job.

So we saw 2.1, 2.2. 2.2 was **शिशुब्राह्मण**. So the first one, 2.1, was called **अजातशत्रुब्राह्मण**. Second is called **शिशुब्राह्मण**, where we saw some similarities between a cow—a calf, really—and **प्राण**, etc. That was supposed to be an explanation of **प्राणा** **वै** **सत्यम्**. Then **तेषामेष** **सत्यम्**. So with that we entered into the third **ब्राह्मण** of the second chapter, which is called **मूर्तामूर्तब्राह्मण**. And though it is called **मूर्तामूर्तब्राह्मण**, what was shown? **वस्तु** is neither **मूर्त** nor **अमूर्त**. **वस्तु** is only known by **नेति** **नेति**. That **नेति** **नेति** was the main thing, the last **मन्त्र** in 2.3. So with that **अजातशत्रुविद्या** ended. **अजातशत्रुविद्या** was **अजातशत्रुब्राह्मण**, **शिशुब्राह्मण** and **मूर्तामूर्तब्राह्मण**. Three **ब्राह्मणाः** was one **विद्या**, one teacher.

Then we entered **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**. **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** was the teaching of **याज्ञवल्क्य** to his wife **मैत्रेयी**. And the highlight was, he said, “I want to take off. I want to go away somewhere, far away.” And therefore that **संन्यासविधि** was highlighted by **भाष्यकार**. He said, “This **विद्या** is **ससंन्यासम्**.” That was highlighted.

All right. So now the next **ब्राह्मण** is beginning, and customary to **भाष्यकार** is to show what is the connection of this **ब्राह्मण** to what has gone by, what this **ब्राह्मण** is going to do. So you can call it **सम्बन्ध**, you can call it **अवतारिका**; it does not matter what we call it. Basically it is a connection, it is a link. At a verse level, we typically call them **अवतारिका**. What is **अवतार**? Coming down. What brings it down is called **अवतारिका**. So bringing it down means what? You are introducing the **मन्त्र** which is about to come.

What is **सम्बन्ध**? Relationship. Relationship between **कर्मकाण्ड** and **ज्ञानकाण्ड**. That is called the basic **सम्बन्धभाष्यम्**. In any **उपनिषद्** you will have a **सम्बन्धभाष्यम्**. What will it be? Because where is **उपनिषद्**? It is at the end of **कर्मकाण्ड**; you have **ज्ञानकाण्ड**. Therefore, what is **ज्ञानकाण्ड**? What is its relevance? Why **कर्मकाण्ड** came before? How is **कर्मकाण्ड** different from **ज्ञानकाण्ड**? These matters are typically taken up in the **सम्बन्धभाष्य**. And once that **सम्बन्धभाष्य** is over, the **उपनिषद्** begins.

So we normally say **सम्बन्धभाष्य** is only one, but sometimes I will use that word to mean the connection between one **ब्राह्मण** and the next also. I say **सम्बन्ध**. You could call it **अवतारिका**, you could call it **सम्बन्ध**, you can just say **भाष्य**. It does not matter what you call it. But every time you begin, there is a connection with what went by and what it connects to the future. These both things can be talked about. And these are places where **भाष्यकार** is extremely brief, but says a lot. It generally requires more attention when we study, because he is saying a lot. You cannot probably say it more succinctly. He covers the whole subject matter, but does it very, very briefly.

All right. So this is what the **मधुब्राह्मण** is to now begin. And we saw towards the end of **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**—in fact, all the way—what is **ब्रह्मविद्या**? **ब्रह्मविद्या** is not “you are consciousness.” That is not **ब्रह्मविद्या**. **ब्रह्मविद्या** is saying **ब्रह्मैवेदं** **सर्वम्**. **इदं** **सर्वम्**, whatever is there, is nothing but **ब्रह्म** **एव**. “But I see many.” Yes, that is why we need **ब्रह्मविद्या**. To see, in spite of seeing many, in spite of experiencing many, **नेह** **नानास्ति** **किञ्चन**. There is no manyness really. In fact, there is no manyness, because you have to know what to count. What should be counted is what is, independently is. So you do not count all derivatives. You count the real thing. If you want to count derivatives, count. That is a different set. But we are interested in counting what really is, what is **सत्यम्**. So to count **सत्यम्**, if you are counting many, we have to just show that the many that you count do not have an independent being.

All right? So here, this you will see at least in the beginning. In **मधुब्राह्मण**, in the beginning, it is just stating this. It is not like an inquiry directly. It is a different way. But what is being done? Again, you basically have to show. All right, you said, “Okay, it is all born of that. It is sustained by that, goes back to that.” But what is that? Again, you bring back and say that which is recognized is everything. Everything is that. That **सर्वात्मभाव** has to be shown.

So what is that **सर्वात्मभाव**? You can show it in different ways. **सर्वात्मभाव** predominantly we show by **कार्यकारणप्रक्रिया**. We say one is the **कारण**; everything else is **कार्य**. **कार्य** has no being independent of **कारणम्**. That is the way it is normally done. All right. So this is the general background for **मधुब्राह्मण**. And in the end of **मधुब्राह्मण**, after having done about fourteen or fifteen **मन्त्राः**, then we will see another story within **मधुब्राह्मण**, four or five **मन्त्राः**. And that packs a lot in it. Actually that last part packs a lot. There you will see some very famous statements which we hear often in the **श्रुति**, presented as a story, **आख्यायिका**, within that. So the first part does not look like an **आख्यायिका**. It is just **श्रुति** is saying something; it looks like it. So now, with that background, we will enter **मधुब्राह्मण**, the commentary.

So **भाष्यकार** begins: **यत्** **केवलं** **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्** **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**, **तद्वक्तव्यमिति** **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मणमारब्धम्**. **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** or **मधुब्राह्मण**? **भाष्यकार** is not like me, okay? I sent a mail saying we are starting **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** today instead of saying **मधुब्राह्मण**. But **भाष्यकार** is not doing that. He is much better than me, obviously. So he said what? **यत्** **केवलं** **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्** **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**, **तद्वक्तव्यमिति** **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मणमारब्धम्**. He is rewinding. He is reminding us why was **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** begun. **यत्** **केवलम्**. **केवलम्**, **अद्वयम्**, only one reality. What is it? **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्**.

See, **ब्रह्मविद्या** is what? **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**. What is **अमृतत्व**? How do you know **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**? “I am going to take off.” **याज्ञवल्क्य** said, “I am going to take off.” What are you going to take off for? What are you going to take off for? **सा** **होवाच** **मैत्रेयी**. And “I am going to give you all the wealth.” **यन्नु** **म** **इयं** **भगोः** **सर्वा** **पृथिवी** **वित्तेन** **पूर्णा** **स्यात्कथं** **तेनामृता** **स्यामिति**. Will I get **अमृतत्वम्**? What did **याज्ञवल्क्य** say? **नेति** **होवाच** **याज्ञवल्क्यः**. No, you will not get it. **अमृतत्वस्य** **तु** **नाशास्ति** **वित्तेन**. Not even a hope, not even a glimmer of hope that wealth will give you **अमृतत्वम्**. Then **येनाहं** **नामृता** **स्यां** **किमहं** **तेन** **कुर्याम्**? What will I do with that? **भगवान्**, **यदेव** **भगवान्** **वेद** **तदेव** **मे** **ब्रवीतु**. No, **ब्रूहीति**. What you know about **अमृतत्व**, that is all you tell me. Because why are you going away? You are going away to firm up your **अमृतत्व**. Because **उद्यास्यन्**—**उद्यास्यन्** **वा** **अहम्** **अस्मि**. For what? For gaining **ज्ञाननिष्ठा**. That is why you are going away. That is what I want.

So what is **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** for? What is the purpose of **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**? **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**. It is **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**. See how briefly he is saying it. **अमृतत्वसाधनं** **वक्तव्यम्**. And that **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**, to gain that abidance in **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**, what is **याज्ञवल्क्य** doing? He is not getting married to a third wife. He is walking away. Why is he walking away? Because this **ज्ञानम्** is **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्**. **अमृतत्वम्** is **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्**, which means what? **कर्म** has nothing to do with **मोक्ष**, once the **अधिकारित्वम्** is there. I should add that. Once an **अधिकारी** is there. I mean, really, **मोक्ष** has got nothing to do with anything, because **मोक्ष** is **सिद्धवस्तु**. But coming from **अविद्यावस्था**, you require **साधनाः**. Otherwise, **सिद्धम्**. Already **सिद्धम्**. Everything is there in **ब्रह्मन्**. What has to be done?

**केवलम्**, **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्**. It has no dependency on **कर्म**. **अमृतत्वसाधनम्**, **तद्वक्तव्यम्**. That has to be said. Because though **अजातशत्रुब्राह्मण** told you **नेति** **नेति**, it did not tell you it is **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्** explicitly. **भाष्यकार** looks at **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** as a seal of approval for **संन्यासम्**. That is how **भाष्यकार** looks at it, so he does not forget that. **कर्मनिरपेक्षम्** **अमृतत्वसाधनं** **तद्वक्तव्यमिति** **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मणम्**. So what is **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण** about?
