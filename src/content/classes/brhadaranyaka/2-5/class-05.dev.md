ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

**मधुब्राह्मण** presents **मधु** as **कार्यम्**, **परस्परकार्यम्**, **परस्परोपकार्योपकारकम्**, **परस्परभोक्तृभोज्यम्**—various ways of looking at it. Basically you are talking of two things which work with each other. Independently they don't have much good. Why is this being presented? To say that whenever two things are mutually depending on each other, neither of them is an absolute truth, because two things cannot mutually depend on each other—they are not two independent things. Therefore the **मिथ्यात्वम्** of whatever is called as **मधु** is the purpose.

So you show the **आधिभौतिक** **प्रपञ्च**. In the **पृथिवी** that's outside and **पृथिवी** that's in the **शरीर**, there is a dependence. In the water outside and water in the **शरीर**, there is a dependence. In the **अग्नितत्त्व** outside, **अग्नितत्त्व** here, there is a dependence—**आधिभौतिक** level. There is also this **अधिभूत**, **अध्यात्म**. Everything is dependent on each other. Everything is backed up by one **चैतन्य**. Therefore the **सूक्ष्मशरीर** has to walk in, and **सूक्ष्मशरीर** is also not **चैतन्य**. It is **चैतन्य**, but it is not **शुद्धचैतन्य**. What else can I say? **सूक्ष्मशरीर** also is one manifestation of **चैतन्य**, and that is manifest both in **अधिभूत** as **देवता**—you know, **पृथिवीदेवता**, **वायुदेवता**—also manifest in each living being as the functionary, as the one who handles the eyes, who handles the nose, who handles the skin, who takes care of, blesses. All of them are what? **परस्परम्**. Everything is supporting everything else. It's not one is supporting the other only; everything is supporting everything else.

Therefore the whole **जगत्** is one noodles. Can't separate them. They are all noodles. What is the truth of the whole noodles? It is one unconvoluted, non-noodle **आत्मा**. This is what has to be shown. This is how it is being done. Instead of just saying **कार्यं** **कारणादभिन्नम्**, here it is saying whatever is **परस्परोपकार्योपकारकम्** is all... And somewhere else you will also hear the word **मधु**. **मधुविद्या** means **ब्रह्मविद्या**, not **विद्या** of—you know, it doesn't mean we are learning about **मिथ्या**. Actually if you learn about **मिथ्या**, **सत्यम्** walks in, because **मिथ्या** cannot be without **सत्यम्**. But understand, when we talk of **मधुविद्या**, it means **ब्रह्मविद्या**. But when we talk of **मधु** in these various verses, it means **कार्यम्**. Okay. All right.

So we were on 2.5. Up to 2.5.10 was following a pattern from 2.5.1. A slight change in the pattern in 2.5.11, which we saw last time. What is **धर्मः**? This is not **आधिभौतिक**, doesn't look like **आध्यात्मिक**. Then what is it? No, it is like that; still here also it is like that. So **अयं** **धर्मः**. What is **धर्म**? So you can see the word **धर्म** and the word **कर्म** are flip sides of one coin. In **गीता** what do you hear? **सर्वधर्मान्** **परित्यज्य** **मामेकं** **शरणं** **व्रज**. What should we do? Give up all your **धर्म**, become an **अधर्मी**? That's not the idea. **सर्वकर्मान्** **परित्यज्य**—**कर्मफलान्** **परित्यज्य** first, then **कर्म**. Can't say **कर्मान्**. **सर्वकर्म** **परित्यज्य**—wrong. So **सर्वकर्म**, because neuter—**पुंलिङ्ग**.

So, you know, **स्वामी** used to say, what happens generally: “**आने** **की** **अर्रम्**, **कुदरे** **की** **गुर्रम्**.” He would say like that. So, you know, if you say **आने**—**आने** apparently in Telugu is called **अर्रम्**. **कुदरे** in Tamil is horse. **आने** in Tamil is elephant; **कुदरे** in Tamil is horse. So what should be the Telugu equivalent? **अर्रम्**, **कुदरे** **की** **गुर्रम्**. Make it that way. So that's what I also did just now. Okay. I said **कर्मान्** is wrong. **धर्मान्** is okay; **कर्मान्** is not okay. So we should be—I should be more alert. All right.

So **धर्म** and **कर्म** are two sides of the same coin. So here **धर्म** means what? **धर्म** is that—it's really this **श्लोक** is telling us that the karmic order, the order of **कर्म** at **व्यष्टि** and **समष्टि** level, is also something. There is a **कर्मफलदाता** and there is a **कर्मकर्ता**. Both of them are **मधु**. **कर्मफलदाता**, **ईश्वरत्वम्**; **कर्मकर्तृत्वम्**, **जीवत्वम्**. Both of them are not absolutely real. Both of them are mutually supporting each other, and therefore they are also **मधु**. All right.

So he is saying that **अयं** **धर्मः** **इति**, **अप्रत्यक्षोऽपि** **धर्मः**. So **धर्म** you can't see. It's not available for you to see. But **धर्म** **अनुष्ठित** as a **कार्य** is available to you to see. So that **अदृष्ट** aspect is being talked about here as **धर्मः**, as though it is **प्रत्यक्ष**. **अप्रत्यक्षोऽपि** **धर्मः** **कार्येण** **तत्प्रयुक्तेन** **प्रत्यक्षेण** **व्यपदिश्यते**. **प्रत्यक्षेण** **व्यपदिश्यते**. It is being mentioned as though it is seen here as **अयं** **धर्मः**, not **तत्** **धर्मः**—**अयं** **धर्मः** **इति** **प्रत्यक्षवत्**. **धर्मश्च** **व्याख्यातः**, **अन्यत्र**, **श्रुतिस्मृतिलक्षणः**—as something which is what is told in **श्रुति** and **स्मृति**. It is **क्षत्त्रादीनामपि** **नियन्ता**, that which even manages or controls the behavior of kings. And it is that due to which the whole **जगत्**—**वैचित्र्य**, the varieties—because of which, that is called **धर्मः**.

And though earlier **सत्य** and **धर्म** is presented as one, now we are saying next verse we will talk of **सत्य**, where **सत्य** is the **अनुष्ठित** **धर्म**. So **धर्म** is—it is known to you, **सत्यं** **वद** **धर्मं** **चर**. You know this, but I tell lies. **सत्य** is not there; **धर्म** is there, **सत्य** is not there. So in that sense the two are shown. And what are both? Both are **मधु**, **सर्वस्य** **मधु**, **सर्वेषां** **मधु**. So that is that we saw last time.

So in that also we saw there is the **सामान्य**, **विशेष**. So you see the **पृथिवीकार्य**—that is the **सामान्यकार्य** of **धर्म**, and each body, each mind is **विशेषकार्य** of **धर्म**. So **सामान्य**, **विशेष** is the distance here between the two, at the two levels. So they will say **आधिदैविक** is **धर्म** at the level of **पृथिवी**, etc. At the level of human body it is **अध्यात्म**, or any body. **आधिदैविक**. Then next **मन्त्र** is talking about—I should say next **कण्डिका**. If you go very strictly, traditionally each verse in **बृहदारण्यक** is called a **कण्डिका**. So they will say **ब्रह्मकण्डिका**. 1.4.10 is called **ब्रह्मकण्डिका**. That is the way it is referred to.

So **इदं** **सत्यम्**. Now **इदं** **सत्यम्**—it was **अयं** **धर्मः**, now **इदं** **सत्यम्**, neuter. **सर्वेषां** **भूतानां** **मध्वस्य** **सत्यस्य** **सर्वाणि** **भूतानि** **मधु** **यश्चायमस्मिन्सत्ये** **तेजोमयोऽमृतमयः** **पुरुषो** **यश्चायमध्यात्मं** **सात्यः**—**तद्धित**—**तेजोमयोऽमृतमयः** **पुरुषोऽयमेव** **स** **योऽयमात्मेदममृतमिदं** **ब्रह्मेदं** **सर्वम्**. So last part you will find the same everywhere, in every verse. **इदममृतमिदं** **ब्रह्मेदं** **सर्वम्**. **सर्वात्मा** has to come.

**दृष्टेनानुष्ठीयमानेन** **आचाररूपेण** **सत्याख्यो** **भवति**, **स** **एव** **धर्मः**. What was talked about in the previous **कण्डिका** as **धर्म**, **स** **एव** **धर्मः**, **दृष्टेन**. What do you mean with **दृष्टेन**? How do you see **धर्म**? It's not seen as **धर्म** standing there. **अनुष्ठीयमानेन** means **क्रियमाणेन**. **अनुष्ठीयमानेन** **आचाररूपेण**—**आचार** is conduct—**आचाररूपेण** **सत्याख्यो** **भवति**. That is called **सत्यम्**. Everything else is same. **सोऽपि** **द्विप्रकार** **एव**. That **सत्य** is also talked of as two levels, **सामान्यविशेषात्मरूपेण**. **सामान्यम्**, common; **विशेष**, specific, individualized.

So what is **सामान्यम्**? **सामान्यरूपः** **पृथिव्यादिसमवेतः**. Why is the earth the way it is? If there is global warming today, we are all subject to it. There is flood in Nepal; global warming is affecting it. That's what Nepal is saying: “We are not accountable for it. We didn't cause this flood, but we have to bear the consequences.” It's one earth. **सामान्यरूपेण**, for all the **जीव** there is one earth; for all the **जीव** there is one air. Whether the farmer burns stubble in Punjab, the suffering is there in Delhi. You know, the smog is there in Delhi. How do you stop this? Diwali is all over India, and they will say because of Diwali you have all these firecrackers producing more smog. So should I say, “Okay, in a village you can burn; in city you don't burn”? It doesn't work that way. Air is common. It burns in Punjab, smoke travels to Delhi. It doesn't stop there. So what is? It's universal. We all in a way support it.

Therefore here also **सामान्य**, **विशेष**. So **सामान्यम्** is **पृथिव्यादि**. And **विशेष** is why I have squint in one eye—that's **विशेष**. Why your ear is extra sharp—**विशेष**. **बुद्धि** is extra sharp—**विशेष**. All this is **विशेष**. **सामान्य**, **विशेष**, everything is accounted for by what? By **धर्म**, which is **अनुष्ठित**—**सत्यम्**. So in a way, without saying it, **उपनिषद्** is also telling you that the entire **जगत्** is governed by an order. It is made clear in **अन्तर्यामिब्राह्मण**. Here also it's being said that the **कर्म** and the **कर्म** is all what? Everything is what? **ब्रह्मन्**. It's not something which—it is not subjective. He doesn't say anywhere, you know, **रज्जुसर्प** and “**सर्प** is saying.” But they both are **ब्रह्मन्**; there's no doubt about it. Even the possibility of a dream—in dream also there is an order. In dream also you can't find anything if there is no light. In dream also if you are hungry, you have to eat food to satisfy it. There is a dream order. That order is also within the same reality. Same reality means **पारमार्थिक**.

So while we say **मिथ्या** includes both **व्यावहारिक** and **प्रातिभासिक**, **प्रातिभासिक** is also **मिथ्या**. You may call it subjective, untrue, but that is also within **मिथ्या**. And what is **मिथ्या**? It is **सत्य**-**अनन्यम्**, and that **सत्यम्** is **एकमेव**. So that **सत्यम्** here—**सत्यम्**, of course, is, we are talking about **अनुष्ठित** **धर्म**. So that he said is also **सामान्य** and **विशेष**. So **सामान्य** means **पृथिव्यादि**, and **विशेषरूपः** **कार्यकरणसङ्घातसमवेतः**. It's included, it's visible in **कार्यकरणसङ्घात**. **तत्र** **पृथिव्यादिसमवेते** **वर्तमानक्रियारूपे** **सत्ये**. So he is talking of this word, you know, here, **सत्ये** **तेजोमयः**, **यश्चायमस्मिन्सत्ये**. What is that **सत्ये**? He is saying **पृथिव्यादिसमवेते** **वर्तमानक्रियारूपे**. It's the effect of the **कर्म**. And **तथा** **अध्यात्मम्**. And what is that? **कार्यकरणसङ्घातसमवेते** **सत्ये**. And what is that called there? **सत्ये** **भवः** **सात्यः**. This is now what? Therefore **कर्म**, **कर्मफल**, both **धर्म** and **अनुष्ठित** **धर्म**, both are what? **मधु** means everything depends on that, and that depends on everything. Therefore there is also **मधु**. And all of them is what? It is the same **योऽयमात्मा**, which was told in **अजातशत्रुब्राह्मण** and **मैत्रेयीब्राह्मण**, and will be told everywhere else. Same **आत्मा** all of it is.
