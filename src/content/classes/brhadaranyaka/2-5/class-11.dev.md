    <p class="src-mulam">इदं वै तन्मधु दध्यङ्ङाथर्वणोऽश्विभ्यामुवाच । तदेतदृषिः पश्यन्नवोचत् । आथर्वणायाश्विनौ दधीचेऽश्व्यं शिरः प्रत्यैरयतम् । स वां मधु प्रवोचदृतायन्त्वाष्ट्रं यद्दस्रावपि कक्ष्यं वामिति ॥ 2-5-17 ॥</p>
    <p class="src-bhashyam"><span class="src-pratika">इदं वै तन्मध्वि</span>त्यादि पूर्ववत् मन्त्रान्तरप्रदर्शनार्थम् । तथा अन्यो मन्त्रः तामेव आख्यायिकामनुसरति स्म । आथर्वणो दध्यङ्नाम — आथर्वणोऽन्यो विद्यत इत्यतो विशिनष्टि — दध्यङ्नाम आथर्वणः, तस्मै <span class="src-pratika">दधीचे</span> <span class="src-pratika">आथर्वणाय</span>, हे <span class="src-pratika">अश्विनावि</span>ति मन्त्रदृशो वचनम् ; <span class="src-pratika">अश्व्यम्</span> अश्वस्य स्वभूतम् , <span class="src-pratika">शिरः</span>, ब्राह्मणस्य शिरसि च्छिन्ने अश्वस्य शिरश्छित्त्वा ईदृशमतिक्रूरं कर्म कृत्वा अश्व्यं शिरः ब्राह्मणं <span class="src-pratika">प्रति</span> <span class="src-pratika">ऐरयतं</span> गमितवन्तौ, युवाम् ; <span class="src-pratika">स</span> च आथर्वणः <span class="src-pratika">वां</span> युवाभ्याम् तन्म<span class="src-pratika">धु</span> <span class="src-pratika">प्रवोचत्</span> , यत्पूर्वं प्रतिज्ञातम् — वक्ष्यामीति । स किमर्थमेवं जीवितसन्देहमारुह्य प्रवोचदित्युच्यते — <span class="src-pratika">ऋतायन्</span> यत्पूर्वं प्रतिज्ञातं सत्यं तत्परिपालयितुमिच्छन् ; जीवितादपि हि सत्यधर्मपरिपालना गुरुतरेत्येतस्य लिङ्गमेतत् । किं तन्मधु प्रवोचदित्युच्यते — <span class="src-pratika">त्वाष्ट्रम्</span> , त्वष्टा आदित्यः, तस्य सम्बन्धि — यज्ञस्य शिरश्छिन्नं त्वष्ट्रा अभवत् , तत्प्रतिसन्धानार्थं प्रवर्ग्यं कर्म, तत्र प्रवर्ग्यकर्माङ्गभूतं यद्विज्ञानं तत् त्वाष्ट्रं मधु — यत्तस्य चिरश्छेदनप्रतिसन्धानादिविषयं दर्शनं तत् त्वाष्ट्रं यन्मधु ; हे <span class="src-pratika">दस्रौ</span> दस्राविति परबलानामुपक्षपयितारौ शत्रूणां हिंसितारौ ; अपि च न केवलं त्वाष्ट्रमेव मधु कर्मसम्बन्धि युवाभ्यामवोचत् ; अपि च <span class="src-pratika">कक्ष्यं</span> गोप्यं रहस्यं परमात्मसम्बन्धि यद्विज्ञानं मधु मधुब्राह्मणेनोक्तं अध्यायद्वयप्रकाशितम् , तच्च वां युवाभ्यां प्रवोचदित्यनुवर्तते ॥</p>
    <p class="src-mulam">इदं वै तन्मधु दध्यङ्ङाथर्वणोऽश्विभ्यामुवाच । तदेतदृषिः पश्यन्नवोचत् । पुरश्चक्रे द्विपदः पुरश्चक्रे चतुष्पदः । पुरः स पक्षी भूत्वा पुरः पुरुष आविशदिति । स वा अयं पुरुषः सर्वासु पूर्षु पुरिशयो नैनेन किञ्चनानावृतं नैनेन किञ्चनासंवृतम् ॥ 2-5-18 ॥</p>
    <p class="src-bhashyam"><span class="src-pratika">इदं वै तन्मध्विति</span> पूर्ववत् । उक्तौ द्वौ मन्त्रौ प्रवर्ग्यसम्बन्ध्याख्यायिकोपसंहर्तारौ ; द्वयोः प्रवर्ग्यकर्मार्थयोरध्याययोरर्थ आख्यायिकाभूताभ्यां मन्त्राभ्यां प्रकाशितः । ब्रह्मविद्यार्थयोस्त्वध्याययोरर्थ उत्तराभ्यामृग्भ्यां प्रकाशयितव्य इत्यतः प्रवर्तते । यत् कक्ष्यं च मधु उक्तवानाथर्वणो युवाभ्यामित्युक्तम् — किं पुनस्तन्मध्वित्युच्यते — <span class="src-pratika">पुरश्चक्रे</span>, <span class="src-pratika">पुरः</span> पुराणि शरीराणि — यत इयमव्याकृत-व्याकरण-प्रक्रिया — स परमेश्वरो नामरूपे अव्याकृते व्याकुर्वाणः प्रथमं भूरादीन् लोकान्सृष्ट्वा, <span class="src-pratika">चक्रे</span> कृतवान् , <span class="src-pratika">द्विपदः</span> द्विपादुपलक्षितानि मनुष्य-शरीराणि पक्षि-शरीराणि ; तथा <span class="src-pratika">पुरः</span> शरीराणि <span class="src-pratika">चक्रे</span> <span class="src-pratika">चतुष्पदः</span> चतुष्पादुपलक्षितानि पशु-शरीराणि ; <span class="src-pratika">पुरः</span> पुरस्तात् , <span class="src-pratika">स</span> ईश्वरः <span class="src-pratika">पक्षी</span> लिङ्गशरीरं <span class="src-pratika">भूत्वा पुरः</span> शरीराणि <span class="src-pratika">— पुरुषः आविशत्</span> इत्यस्यार्थमाचष्टे श्रुतिः — <span class="src-pratika">स वा अयं पुरुषः सर्वासु पूर्षु</span> सर्वशरीरेषु <span class="src-pratika">पुरिशयः</span>, पुरि शेत इति, पुरिशयः सन् पुरुष इत्युच्यते ; <span class="src-pratika">न</span> <span class="src-pratika">एनेन</span> अनेन <span class="src-pratika">किञ्चन</span> किञ्चिदपि <span class="src-pratika">अनावृतम्</span> अनाच्छादितम् ; तथा <span class="src-pratika">न एनेन किञ्चनासंवृतम्</span> अन्तरननुप्रवेशितम् — बाह्यभूतेनान्तर्भूतेन च न अनावृतम् ; एवं स एव नामरूपात्मना अन्तर्बहिर्भावेन कार्यकरणरूपेण व्यवस्थितः ; पुरश्चक्रे इत्यादिमन्त्रः सङ्क्षेपत आत्मैकत्वमाचष्ट इत्यर्थः ॥</p>
    <p class="src-mulam">इदं वै तन्मधु दध्यङ्ङाथर्वणोऽश्विभ्यामुवाच । तदेतदृषिः पश्यन्नवोचत् । रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बभूव तदस्य रूपं प्रतिचक्षणाय । इन्द्रो मायाभिः पुरुरूप ईयते युक्ता ह्यस्य हरयः शता दशेति । अयं वै हरयोऽयं वै दश च सहस्राणि बहूनि चानन्तानि च तदेतद्ब्रह्मापूर्वमनपरमनन्तरमबाह्यमयमात्मा ब्रह्म सर्वानुभूरित्यनुशासनम् ॥ 2-5-19 ॥</p>

ॐ श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणानाम् आलयं करुणालयम्। नमामि भगवत्पादं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम्॥ शङ्करं शङ्कराचार्यं केशवं बादरायणम्। सूत्रभाष्यकृतौ वन्दे भगवन्तौ पुनः पुनः॥ ईश्वरो गुरुरात्मेति मूर्तिभेदविभागिने। व्योमवद्व्याप्तदेहाय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः॥ ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥ ॐ नमो ब्रह्मादिभ्यो ब्रह्मविद्यासंप्रदायकर्तृभ्यो वंशऋषिभ्यो नमो गुरुभ्यः॥

So we are in the midst of the — not midst — almost the end of the **Madhu Brāhmaṇa**. So we are looking at the last four verses, which, through an **आख्यायिका**, is telling us something. So among them, the first two verses we saw, almost: sixteen and seventeen. So the story is what? That the **Aśvinī** **Kumāras** wanted **ब्रह्मविद्या**. They went to **Dadhyaṅ** **Maharṣi**, and **Dadhyaṅ** **Maharṣi** said, "You know, I can't teach you, because if I accept you as a student, Indra will take my head off." They said, "We'll take care of you." "How will you take care of me?" "We are royal physicians; we know how to do things. So we will first replace your head with the head of a horse. And then, as soon as you accept, we'll do that. Then, when you start teaching, Indra will come and take out the head of the horse. Then he'll put your real head back, and you're back to normal." This is the deal, and he agreed.

So he had to undergo two surgeries. And that was presented as a **स्तुति**, to say that, you know, how much effort is taken by the student to secure this knowledge, and how much the teacher is committed to keeping the truth that he will teach: despite all these, taking all the risks, he taught. So it's a very, very glorious **विद्या**. That's one **स्तुति**. So he says — you know, the mantra says — "I am going to announce to everybody what you did. Nobody else knows; this is a secret deal, but we will tell everybody." Why will you tell? So that **स्तुति** happens. That is the idea. Okay.

**इदं वै तन्मधु दध्यङ्ङाथर्वणोऽश्विभ्यामुवाच । तदेतदृषिः पश्यन्नवोचत् । आथर्वणायाश्विनौ दधीचेऽश्व्यं शिरः प्रत्यैरयतम् । स वां मधु प्रवोचदृतायन्त्वाष्ट्रं यद्दस्रावपि कक्ष्यं वामिति ॥**

So now the benefit is you can look at it with **सन्धि**, without **सन्धि**, with ease. So if you look with **सन्धि**, it shows you, you know, where each section ends, etcetera. Nice to see that. **जीवितादपि हि सत्यधर्मपरिपालना गुरुतरेत्येतस्य लिङ्गमेतत्** — why, why this story has to tell us this **ऋतायन्**? Why is this an extra adjective? So he says: to show what? **जीवितादपि सत्यधर्मपरिपालना गुरुतरा**. **जीवितात्**: even more than living. More than just being alive, what is important? **सत्यधर्मपरिपालना गुरुतरा**.

What does **गुरुतरा** mean now? **गुरुतरा** means even more: more important, more heavy, in a way. So these numbers now refer to the **Govinda Prasādinī** **टीका** of **Kailāsa** **Āśrama**. So you can even have that now. You know, we can go look at — so pretty much everything in the **Kailāsa** **Āśrama** books is also here now, in this site, which is really wonderful. So why this **ऋतायन्** word is used: to show that, you know, with a desire to keep the truth, "I will teach you," taking all the risk, he did this. This means he is showing the importance of truth.

You see a similar thing in **Taittirīya**: **सत्यान्न प्रमदितव्यम्**. Never go away from truth. What did you see in **Kaṭhopaniṣad**? There **Naciketā** tells his father, **सस्यमिव मर्त्यः पच्यते सस्यमिवाजायते पुनः**: a human body is born and goes like corn plants, you know, plants which come up and go. "Come on, keep your truth." Tells his father, "Keep the truth. You promised something, right? I am giving you the breath: send me to **भगवान् यम**." Again the importance of **सत्यम्**. Because what you hold on to is this body — is nothing; it will go away. Hold on to the truth. And that truth, again, because the whole pursuit of **शास्त्र** is nothing but the pursuit of truth. So if you are not interested in truth, it's not going to bless you. Therefore hold on to the truth. That idea is being said; therefore it is said, it is **लिङ्गम्**. So. Then — yeah, then what?

**किं तन्मधु प्रवोचदित्युच्यते — त्वाष्ट्रम्** — what is the **मधु** he taught? First one, **त्वाष्ट्रम्**. What is **त्वाष्ट्रम्**? **त्वष्टा आदित्यः**. **Śaṅkarācārya** is explaining the word. **त्वष्टा** is **आदित्य**. Therefore what is **त्वाष्ट्रम्**? **तद्धित**. So it's **तस्य सम्बन्धि**. So you can see that: **तस्य**, **षष्ठी**, **सम्बन्धि**; you don't need any more thing, okay. **तस्य सम्बन्धि**. So, **तस्य सम्बन्धि**.

**यज्ञस्य शिरश्छिन्नं त्वष्ट्रा अभवत्** — so basically some **यज्ञपुरुष** — this story I told you last time; I don't know — **यज्ञपुरुष**'s head is gone, and that head has to be put back for things to happen properly. And for that reason, **तत्प्रतिसन्धानार्थं प्रवर्ग्यं कर्म**. What is this **प्रवर्ग्यं कर्म**? Putting back the head of **यज्ञपुरुष** back on the neck. For that reason: **प्रतिसन्धानार्थं** **कर्म**. So, what does it say? **त्वष्टृरूपशिरः प्रतिसंधानार्थमिति भावः**. So, for the sake of that. And what is **यज्ञपुरुष**? **यज्ञो वै विष्णुः**. There's some story which we don't know enough about. All right? So:

**तत्र प्रवर्ग्यकर्माङ्गभूतं यद्विज्ञानं तत् त्वाष्ट्रं मधु** — so, as part of that **प्रवर्ग्यकर्म**, wherein the head of the **यज्ञपुरुष** has to be refixed, there is some **उपासना** needed. And that **उपासना** is called **यद्विज्ञानम्** here. Okay, how do I know this **उपासना**? This is where this **टिप्पणी** can help at times. You go there; he will tell you, **उपासनम्**. A little, you know, poetic there. The whole idea is this: I am; **आत्मा** is **पुरुष**. That's why **भाष्यकार** doesn't say much here, but very, very, very beautiful mantras, very often quoted; you hear this all the time. **पुरि शेते पुरुषः**: you hear this so often. But — and that one alone became everything. Why did he become everything? So that you recognise how glorious the truth is.

This is the **विभूतियोग** of the **Gītā**. What is **विभूति**? Everything. "Everything is myself." "What? Tell me how. What is everything?" "You want to know?" **भगवान्** started the list. First he said, "Everything is me." You don't understand that? "Now I will give you a few." Went on and on. Arjuna is not happy: "Show me." **चलो, देख लो**: **विश्वरूपदर्शन**. All for what? To show that **महिमा** of **ईश्वर**. And that **महिमा** is your **महिमा**. So what is this? Where does this **महिमा** — where is the place for this **महिमा**? You can either say it's in himself, or you can say there is no **महिमा**: **स्वे महिम्नि** or **न महिम्नि**: **Chāndogya**. Because **महिमा** means you are a small fellow looking at **महिमा**. If you understand the truth, there is no **महिमा**. **स्वे महिम्नि** or **न महिम्नि**: both are okay.

So, very — just look at the mantra again, okay. So now this is where I saw, you know: I am **आत्मा**, **ब्रह्म**. And what is the nature of this **ब्रह्म**? **तदेतद्ब्रह्म**, is what? **अपूर्वम्**. Let me put **पदच्छेद**, okay? Easier for you to see. **अपूर्वम् अनपरम् अनन्तरम् अबाह्यम्**: four words. What is **अपूर्वम्**? That which had no predecessor; which means what? It has no **कारण**. It has no **कारण**. This **जगत्** is **कार्यम्**. **अनपरम्**: it has no **कार्य**. What is the whole — this **कार्यकारण**, everything you talked about, just talked about it? There is no real **कार्य** or **कारण**. Really there is no **कार्य और कारण**. It's only one. Like your dream: there is no **कार्यकारण**; there is only one. Appears, disappears.

**अपूर्वम्**, **अनपरम्**; **अनन्तरम्**: it's not inside; **अबाह्यम्**: it's not outside. It's both. Because if you look at it spatially — it's not inside, not outside — then where is it? Then it doesn't exist? It exists. It is not inside; it's not outside; it exists. It's not before; it's not after; it exists. It's not a **कार्य**, it's not a **कारण**; it exists. Whole world is **कार्यकारण** to us. You have to — these are all like the Zen poems, you know. It has to stun you into stop thinking the old way. You have to drop the old way of trying to know it as one more thing. That tendency — how do you break? By these words, which are seemingly paradoxical: **अपूर्वम् अनपरम् अनन्तरम् अबाह्यम्**. Then how do you know this? **ब्रह्म सर्वानुभूः**.

**सर्वानुभूः**. There is no perception, no experience, without it. You want Brahman experience? Every experience is Brahman experience. Experiencer is Brahman, experienced is Brahman, experience is Brahman. What else is there? There is only Brahman. **सर्वानुभूः**: **अनुभू**, **अनुभूतिस्वरूपः**. You want an experience? Enjoy: every experience is Brahman experience. "No, no, I want special experience." **छोड़ दो उसको।** Special experience you can have for some time, just to connect. It's not a special experience. It better be an experience where you recognise this super special knowledge, or the most general knowledge, whichever way you want to look at it. **सर्वानुभूः**: that again is a very common word.

See these words: **अपूर्वम्**, **अनपरम्**, **अनन्तरम्**. **अपूर्वम् अनपरम्**: very commonly quoted by **भाष्यकार** here and there. In **वार्तिक** you find a lot of it. Because he wants to say it is neither **कार्यम्** nor **कारणम्**, because the entire **प्रक्रिया** — we spend so much effort establishing **कारणकार्यम्**. And then we say **कार्य** is not different from **कारण**. Now **कारण** remains, you know. If you said everything is effect and cause, finally you will be stuck with the cause. That causal status is **मिथ्या**. And that is said very eloquently by saying it is not a **कार्यम्** — **अनपरम्** — and it is not a **कारण** — **अपूर्वम्**. Therefore it neither — see, if a **कारण** becomes a **कार्य**, then **कारण** is changing. If it is changing, then it is **अनित्यम्**. If Brahman becomes the **जगत्**, it's **अनित्यम्**. If it's **अनित्यम्**, we don't care for it, because we are interested in **नित्यम्**. If Brahman itself — **जगत्कारणम्** — it became, then it won't work.

Therefore what kind of **कारणम्** is it? **विवर्तोपादानकारणम्**: without changing, it appears to change. Therefore it is **अपूर्वम्**. Where did it come from? Brahman. Okay, **जगत्** came from Brahman. Where did Brahman come from? You don't extend it further. It is **अपूर्वम्**. At the same time, did it become the world? It didn't: **अनपरम्**. It neither became — it's neither a **कारण** nor a **कार्य**; it's neither a cause nor an effect. But then what is the whole problem? The whole problem is this one alone — what did it do? **रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बभूव**. Again, very quoted — commonly quoted.

It became — as though became — every single name and form. **रूपं रूपं प्रतिरूपो बभूव**. Why did he do this? **अस्य रूपं प्रतिचक्षणाय**: to announce how brilliant it is, how — you know, what should I say — multifaceted it is. **प्रतिरूप**, you know; **प्रतिचक्षणाय**. To — **चक्षण** is to speak, okay: **चक्षिङ्**. To present its glory. What did he do to present its glory? This **ईश्वरः**, **इन्द्रः**: **इन्द्रो मायाभिः पुरुरूप ईयते**. **मायाभिः** — we'll see in the **भाष्य**. Why is **माया**? He took — **पुरु** means **बहु** — became many forms. See, he as though has got horses. **हरि** has also the meaning of horse.

**युक्ता ह्यस्य हरयः**: next part. **युक्ता ह्यस्य हरयः**: he has got all these horses. "Got" means not — **युक्त** means yoked: horses are yoked to the cart, you know. **युक्ता ह्यस्य हरयः**. And how many of them? **शता दश**: hundreds of them. What are these horses? **अयं वै हरयः**. **अयं वै दश च सहस्राणि बहूनि चानन्तानि च**. What is **हरयः**? You tell me. Why — what? What makes you see the world is manifold? What makes you see manyness? The mind and senses. The mind and senses. So the mind and senses, the **इन्द्रिय**s, are called **हरयः**. What do they do? **हरन्ति**. **हरन्ति प्रसभं मनः**. They carry you away; they take you away from your truth. **हरयः**: **हरन्ति इति हरयः**. Because **पराञ्चि खानि व्यतृणत्स्वयम्भूः**. So all of these quotations will make sense if you heard those **उपनिषद्**s, okay? But **Kaṭhopaniṣad** many of you have listened: **पराञ्चि खानि**.

By making your senses turned outward, he has, as though, destroyed you. Who? This **स्वयम्भूः**: **स्वयं भवति सर्वम्**. But he has, as though, destroyed us. By what? Turned outward. Same **हरयः**. How many of them? Ten. Why ten? Hundred. Why hundred? Thousand. "Hey, how are there thousand?" Look at all the beings: earthworm sense, a bacteria sense, your sense, a plant sense — are all different senses. How many varieties of senses are there in different living organisms? All of them are there: **युक्ता ह्यस्य हरयः**. How did they all come about? **मायाभिः**. Not really there, okay? They all came about. **युक्ता ह्यस्य हरयः**; **मायाभिः**, **पुरुरूप ईयते**.

"So all these senses are other than me?" No, that is also I am: **पुरुषः एव**. This **पुरुष** alone is also those **इन्द्रिय**s. Means what? This world, the physical body, **सूक्ष्मशरीर**, what — **कारणशरीर**, **मायाभिः**: everything is but that Brahman alone. Who is what? **अपूर्वम् अनपरम् अनन्तरम् अबाह्यम्**. How do I know him? Don't look for "how do I know him". **सर्वानुभूः**: recognise him in every experience. What more can be said about the **उपनिषद्**, you know? That's this mantra.
